## Historical situation of the imprint

- Kirchner was **court preacher to Elector Palatine Ludwig VI (Lutheran)** at Heidelberg from ~1579.
- Ludwig VI died **12 October 1583** — one month after the Vorrede's dateline (6 Sept 1583).
- Under regent Johann Casimir, the Palatinate immediately re-**Reformed** (returned to Reformed confession) after Ludwig's death.
- The *Enchiridion* was thus published in the final month of the Palatinate's brief Lutheran interlude, dedicated to the very Elector whose death would ensure its printing region reverted to Reformed rule within weeks.
- Its double polemical target — **Papists AND Sacramentarians (Reformed)** — makes historical sense: Heidelberg 1583 sat between them.
- Kirchner also co-authored (with Chemnitz and Selnecker) the **1583 *Apologia oder Verantwortung des christlichen Concordien-Buchs*** defending the Formula/Book of Concord. The *Enchiridion* Vorrede (cv 27) explicitly binds him to "the confession of the saving faith **repeated in the Christian Book of Concord**."

## Latin equivalent

- Kirchner published a Latin systematic version: *Methodica explicatio praecipuorum capitum doctrinae coelestis* (1586 Wipprecht, 1310 pp; 1586 Steinman; 1595 Beyer, 1029 pp). Google Books has all three (direct PDF download captcha-blocked). Not a translation of the *Enchiridion* strictly — a fuller Latin systematic expansion. A future *Methodica* volume in the corpus could stand as its companion.

## OCR quality

- BSB per-canvas hOCR is ~85% accurate for this 1583 Fraktur — well above the typical Fraktur floor.
- Common OCR errors: long-s (ſ) vs f confusion; ie/ce/re at word ends; hyphenated line-break dashes lost; occasional "0" for "o".
- Cross-referenced against known citations (Ps 19, 2 Tim 3, Nehemiah 4, etc.) to validate readings.

## Editorial conventions applied

- Normalise Kirchner's 1583 orthography: **vnd → und**, **vnnd → und** (emphatic where marked), **vff → auff → upon**, **ff → ff**, **ck → ck**, long-s silently modernised, terminal-*ẑ* preserved as *ß* in German citations.
- Ecclesiastical Latin quotations (Athanasius, Augustine, Chalcedon) preserved verbatim from Kirchner, glossed in the English.
- Bible citations: preserve Kirchner's citation form (e.g. **Erod. 32** = Exodus 32) in body; give modern reference in endnote where the abbreviation differs from modern usage.
