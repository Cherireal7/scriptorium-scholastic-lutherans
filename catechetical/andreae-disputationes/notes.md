# Jakob Andreae, *Theological Disputations* — Working Notes

## Volume architecture

- One continuous master translation: `review/andreae-disputationes.md`.
- One collected DOCX and PDF for the entire disputation corpus.
- Disputations remain internally identified by number, title, date, and respondent, but are not published as separate translation files.
- Source facsimiles may be grouped by witness under `sources-original/`; these are evidentiary files, not separate deliverables.

## Disputation I — *De norma veritatis* (1588)

## Scope

Complete translation of the independent six-page disputation (four leaves including the title leaf), not an excerpt from the later collected *Disputationes theologicae*.

## Text base

- Badische Landesbibliothek Karlsruhe, shelf mark 60 A 2690 R.
- Regensburg: Johann Burger, 1588.
- VD16 ZV 583; URN `urn:nbn:de:bsz:31-128697`.
- Public Domain Mark 1.0.
- Local facsimile and BLB OCR stored under `sources-original/`.

## Collation decisions

- The BLB OCR is heavily corrupted by long-*s*, ligatures, and hyphenated line endings. All substantive text was checked visually against PDF images 4 and 6–10.
- Thesis 3 reads *Beroeensibus* and refers to Paul and Silas (Acts 17:11).
- Thesis 4 reads *in Apostolorum doctrinam, quae indubitata erat*; “which was beyond doubt” preserves the argumentative force.
- Thesis 10 prints Augustine’s older citation as “Epist. 19 ad Hieronymum”; modern numbering is Epistle 82.3. The first quotation is *Contra Maximinum* II.14.3.
- Thesis 12 marginally identifies “Chusani epistola 2. de usu communionis.” The English retains Andreae’s three extracted propositions and typographical emphasis without attempting to reconstruct their wider Cusan context in the body.
- The final declaration’s *cum ipso … nequaquam disputandum esse* has been rendered “no disputation whatsoever … is to be held with him,” preserving its procedural force.

## Editorial restraint

The original is exceptionally short. No artificial parts, contents page, glossary, or decorative half-title sequence have been added. The reader-facing apparatus is limited to a source note and twelve notes needed to identify citations or explain editorial decisions.

## Disputation II — *De auctoritate ... et perspicuitate Sacrae Scripturae*

- Text base: Andreae, *Disputationes theologicae* (Montbéliard/Frankfurt, 1593), Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin, PPN 766549208, collection canvases 25–37.
- Fourteen downloaded images include canvas 38 only as the boundary check: canvas 38 begins the next disputation, *De iudice in controversiis*.
- Respondent: M. Christoph Scholtius of Breslau in Silesia; held in 1588.
- Translation progress: complete through thesis 71, including the dedication and concluding transition to the disputation on traditions.
- Thesis 7’s exact chronological claim, 2,461 years of oral transmission before the written Decalogue, has been preserved rather than harmonized with a modern chronology.
