# Andreae & Liebler, *Norma Veritatis* (1588)

### *Theses Theologicae de Norma Veritatis et Iudice Controversiarum Religionis*

*Under the presidency of the Reverend and most illustrious man Jakob Andreae, Doctor of Sacred Theology, most worthy Provost and Chancellor of the Church and Academy of Tübingen, his revered teacher; at the Gymnasium of Regensburg, M. Jakob Liebler, Minister of the Church at Regensburg, will respond on the tenth day of January, 1588. Regensburg: printed by Johann Burger.*

— ❦ —

## Quaestio

Seeing that from the very days of the Apostles down unto the present day sundry **heresies** and contentions have exercised the Church, not without cause is it asked: what is that one **rule of truth** (*unica regula veritatis*) and the fitting **judge** in controversies of religion?

— ❦ —

### Thesis 1.

That the **Sacred Scripture** of the Old and New Testament is the sole rule and norm of truth (*unica regula et norma veritatis*), out of which all controversies in the doctrine of religion are to be judged: this is placed beyond all doubt among all truly godly men.

— ❦ —

### Thesis 2.

For *"every man is a liar, but God alone is true in his word"* (**Rom. 3:4**). Hence we read written concerning the Beroeans, that after they had with all eagerness received the word of the Lord preached by Paul and Silas, *"they daily searched the Scriptures, whether these things were so"* — even in the very manner in which they had been preached by the Apostles (**Acts 17:11**).

— ❦ —

### Thesis 3.

For if it was lawful to inquire into the Apostles' doctrine, which was itself undoubted, and to take judgment out of the Prophetic writings, then much more of necessity must all the writings of all doctors in every age be subjected, as unto a judge, to the writings of the **Prophets and Apostles**; according to that saying of Paul: *"If an angel from heaven should preach any other gospel [unto you than that which we have preached], let him be accursed"* (**Gal. 1:8**).

— ❦ —

### Thesis 4.

Hither belong the illustrious testimonies of the Prophets and Apostles: *"To the law and to the testimony; if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them"* (**Isa. 8:20**). Item: *"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path"* (**Ps. 119:105**). Item: *"This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him"* (**Matt. 17:5**). Item: *"We have also a more sure word of prophecy, whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn"* (**2 Pet. 1:19**).

— ❦ —

### Thesis 5.

For although certain difficult and obscure things occur in some places of Scripture, yet the doctrine of **salvation** is explained through other, plainer places within the same Scripture.

— ❦ —

### Thesis 6.

Yet by this Judge nothing at all is taken away from the judgment of the **Church**; rather, her authority is confirmed — of which Christ saith: *"If he will not hear the Church, let him be unto thee as an heathen man and a publican"* (**Matt. 18:17**).

— ❦ —

### Thesis 7.

For the Church hath no **absolute power**, but a power bound and constrained unto the word of GOD; according to that saying of Paul: *"If we ourselves (the Apostles) should preach otherwise, we would be anathema"* (**Gal. 1:8**).

— ❦ —

### Thesis 8.

Therefore every judgment of the Church, unless it hath been subjected to the word of the Lord, and drawn out of it, and confirmed by it, is to be received for a **human dream** (*humanum somnium*), and in no wise for a judgment or decree of the Church.

— ❦ —

### Thesis 9.

The very same is likewise the judgment of St. **Augustine** and of the rest of the more sincere Fathers and Doctors of the Church. Augustine [*Contra Maximinum* II.14]: *"But now,"* saith he, *"neither ought I to bring forth the Council of Nicaea, nor thou that of Ariminum, as though about to prejudge the case. I am not held by the authority of this [Arian] bishop, nor thou by that of the other. By the authorities of the Scriptures — not those proper to either party, but common witnesses to both — let matter be conferred with matter, cause with cause, reason with reason."*

— ❦ —

### Thesis 10.

Item [*Epist. 82 ad Hieronymum*]: *"For I confess unto thy charity, that unto those books of the Scriptures alone which are now called Canonical have I learned to yield this fear and honour: that I most firmly believe that no author of them, in writing anything, hath erred. And if in them I shall find anything which seemeth contrary to truth, I shall not doubt but that either the codex is faulty, or the translator hath not followed what was said, or that I myself have not at all understood. But other authors I so read that, however much they may prevail in sanctity and doctrine, I do not therefore hold it true because they themselves thought so, but because they have been able to persuade me — whether by those Canonical authors, or by probable reason — that it doth not depart from the truth. Nor do I judge, my brother, that thou dost think aught otherwise; nor, I say, do I suppose that thou wouldest have thine own books read as those of the Prophets and Apostles, of whose writings — that they are free from all error — to doubt is impious. Far be this from a pious humility and a truthful estimation of thyself."*

— ❦ —

### Thesis 11.

Therefore we affirm that the voice of the colloquists at the **Colloquy of Worms**, put forth by the Papists, is blasphemous and damned — wherein they said: *"Sacred Scripture is not the voice of the judge, but the matter of the suit"* (*non vocem iudicis, sed materiam litis*).

— ❦ —

### Thesis 12.

No less blasphemous and execrable do we by no means doubt the sentence of **Cardinal Cusanus** (*Chusanus*) also to be, when he saith [*Epist. 7*]: *"Let it not move thee, that the Sacred Scriptures were adapted to their time and variously understood, so that at one time they were expounded according to the currently prevailing universal rite: THE RITE BEING CHANGED, LET THE INTERPRETATION ALSO BE CHANGED."* Item: *"The Scriptures pertain to the well-being of the Church's government even when it hath been begun and continued, but they can in no wise exist of its essence."* Item: *"THE JUDGMENT of the Church BEING CHANGED, the judgment of GOD is CHANGED also."*

— ❦ —

### Thesis 13.

Whosoever, therefore, doth not acknowledge Sacred Scripture alone (*sola Scriptura*) for the one and most sufficient Judge of the controversies of religion, but refuseth it — him we pronounce both to distrust his own cause, and that with such a one in the business of religion there is by no means any disputing to be had.

— ❦ —
