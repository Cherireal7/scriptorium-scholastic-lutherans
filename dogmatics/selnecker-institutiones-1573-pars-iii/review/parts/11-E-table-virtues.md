# *Tabella, continens appellationes virtutum, et earum extrema, iuxta seriem praeceptorum Decalogi* · A Table Containing the Names of the Virtues, and Their Extremes, According to the Order of the Commandments of the Decalogue

The virtues, or good works, which are contained in the several commandments stand in the mean. But the extremes, in the defect, which is ***ἔλλειψις*** (a falling short), and in the excess, which is ***ὑπερβολή*** (an overshooting), are vicious, and show the sins against the several commandments and against each virtue of them. Now the definitions of the virtues and vices, illustrated by an exposition of their causes and by examples, let the young seek in the most learned little book of **D. Chytraeus**, which is entitled *Regulae vitae* (Rules of Life);[^E-1] which little book let them never suffer to be shaken out of their hands, for the sake of its manifold doctrine and godliness.

## *Virtutes primi praecepti* · The Virtues of the First Commandment

1. ***ἐπίγνωσις ἀληθὴς τοῦ θεοῦ*** — *Vera Dei noticia* — The true knowledge of God.
2. ***πίστις*** — *Fides* — Faith.
3. ***ἀγάπη*** — *Dilectio* — Love.
4. ***ἐλπίς*** — *Spes* — Hope.
5. ***φόβος θεοῦ*** — *Timor Dei* — The fear of God.
6. ***ταπείνωσις*** — *Humilitas* — Humility.
7. ***ὑπομονή*** — *Patientia* — Patience.
8. ***ἐπιμονὴ ἢ καρτερία*** — *Perseverantia sive constantia* — Perseverance, or constancy.

### *Extrema* · The Extremes

**The extremes contrary to the knowledge of God are:**

1. ***ἡ ἄγνοια τῆς οὐσίας καὶ βουλήσεως τοῦ θεοῦ*** — *Ignoratio Dei* — Ignorance of God [of the essence and will of God].
2. ***ἀσέβεια*** — *impietas, neglectio, et contemtus Dei* — Ungodliness, the neglect and contempt of God.
3. ***πᾶσαι πλάναι καὶ δόξαι ἀντιπαλαίουσαι τῷ τοῦ θεοῦ λόγῳ*** — *omnes errores et opiniones repugnantes verbo Dei* — All errors and opinions that fight against the Word of God.

**In excess** there is contrary to the knowledge of God that ***κενοσπουδία*** (vain busying), which is curiosity, searching into <!-- p.209 -->the hidden Essence and Will of God not revealed in the Word.

**Contrary to faith are:**

1. ***ἡ ἀπορία περὶ τοῦ θεοῦ, καὶ περὶ τῆς προνοίας, καὶ τῆς διδαχῆς καὶ ἐπαγγελίας*** — *dubitatio de Deo, et de providentia, et doctrina, et promissione* — Doubting concerning God, and concerning providence, and the doctrine, and the promise.
2. ***ἀπιστία καὶ κακοπιστία*** — *incredulitas et falsa fides* — Unbelief and false faith.
3. ***δυσελπιστία*** — *diffidentia* — Distrust.
4. ***ἀπόγνωσις καὶ ἀνελπία*** — *desperatio* — Despair.

**In excess:**

1. ***θράσος καὶ πρόληψις*** — *confidentia et praesumtio* — Overboldness and presumption.
2. ***πεποίθησις τῆς ἀνθρωπίνης βοηθείας*** — *fiducia humani auxilii* — Trust in the help of man.
3. ***εἰδωλολατρεία*** — *Idolorum cultura* — The worship of idols.
4. ***δεισιδαιμονία*** — *superstitio* — Superstition.
5. ***Μαγεία*** — *incantatio* — Enchantment. ***χρησμοὶ ἢ μαντεύματα*** — *oracula* — Oracles. ***θεομαντεία*** — *vaticinia afflatorum, etc.* — The soothsayings of them that are possessed with a spirit, and the like.

**Contrary to love are:**

1. ***ἐπιθυμία, ἢ ἀταξία τῶν παθῶν*** — *concupiscentia, seu omnes pravae inclinationes* — Concupiscence, or all depraved inclinations [the disorder of the affections].
2. ***μῖσος ἢ ἔχθος τοῦ θεοῦ*** — *odium Dei* — Hatred of God.
3. ***βρόμος καὶ ἀγανάκτησις*** — *fremitus et indignatio adversus Deum* — Murmuring and indignation against God.
4. ***ἔκπληξις καὶ πτόησις ἢ ἀναίσθησις ἐν τῇ τοῦ θεοῦ ἀγάπῃ*** — *Stupor non sentiens flammas dilectionis Dei* — A numbness that feeleth not the flames of the love of God.

**In excess:** Superstition.

**Contrary to hope** are the same things, ***τὰ αὐτὰ αἰσχρὰ ἐν τῇ ἐνδείᾳ καὶ ὑπερβολῇ*** (the same shameful things in the defect and in the excess), as are contrary to faith: chiefly, despair and presumption.

**Contrary to the fear of God are:**

1. ***ἄδεια*** — *securitas* — Carnal security.
2. ***ὀλιγωρία ἢ οὐδένεια τοῦ θεοῦ*** — *contemtus Dei* — Contempt of God [the slighting of God, or the setting of Him at nought].
3. ***ἔμπαιγμα τοῦ θείου λόγου*** — *derisio Verbi divini* — Mockery of the divine Word.

**In excess:**

1. ***δεισιδαιμονία*** — *superstitio* — Superstition.
2. ***ὁ φόβος δούλειος*** — *servilis timor, fugiens Deum* — Servile fear, which fleeth from God.

**Contrary to humility are:**

1. ***ἀλαζονεία ἢ ὑπερηφάνεια*** — *superbia, iactantia, fastus* — Pride, boasting, haughtiness.
2. ***φιλαυτία*** — *amor et admiratio sui ipsius* — The love and admiration of oneself.
3. ***καταφρόνησις τοῦ πλησίου*** — *contemtus proximi* — Contempt of one's neighbour.

**In excess:**

1. ***ὑπόκρισις*** — *simulatio humilitatis* — The feigning of humility, such as is that of the Anabaptists and of the monks, ***οἳ θέλουσιν εἶναι ταπεινοὶ ἄνευ ὀλιγωρίας, καὶ πένητες ἄνευ ἐνδείας, καὶ πλούσιοι ἄνευ πόνου, καὶ ἅγιοι μάρτυρες ἄνευ σταυροῦ*** (who would be lowly without being slighted, and poor without want, and rich without labour, and holy martyrs without a cross).
2. ***μικροψυχία*** — *pusillanimitas* — Faintheartedness. For this sinneth in excess against humility; in defect, against hope and fortitude.

<!-- p.210 -->

**Contrary to patience are:** in defect, ***ἀτλησία*** — *impatientia* — impatience; in excess, ***ὑποκριτικὴ ὑπομονή*** — *hypocritica patientia* — hypocritical patience, as in the Hermits.

**Contrary to perseverance are:**

1. ***ἀποστασία*** — *defectio* — Falling away.
2. ***ἰσχυρογνωμοσύνη*** — *pertinacia* — Stubbornness.
3. ***τὸ ἀπαλγεῖν τῶν ἀπηλγηκότων*** — *stupor eorum, qui dedoluerunt* — The numbness of them that are past feeling.
4. ***σκάνδαλον ἢ διδόμενον, ἢ λαμβανόμενον καὶ φαρισαϊκόν*** — *offensio, et scandalum, sive datum, sive acceptum* — Offence and stumbling-block, whether given, or taken [and Pharisaical].

## *Virtutes II. Praecepti* · The Virtues of the Second Commandment

1. ***τὸ ὀρθῶς θεοκλητεῖν καὶ ἐπικαλεῖσθαι*** — *Invocare Deum* — To call upon God [aright]. ***προσκύνησις*** — *adoratio* — Adoration. ***προσευχή*** — *petitio* — Petition. ***δέησις καὶ αἴτησις*** — *oratio* — Prayer.
2. ***εὐχαριστία*** — *gratiarum actio* — Thanksgiving.
3. ***ἀληθὴς διδαχή, ἢ ἀληθὲς κήρυγμα*** — *puritas doctrinae et vera praedicatio* — Purity of doctrine and true preaching.
4. ***ὁμολόγησις*** — *Confessio* — Confession.
5. ***ὅρκος ἐπακτὸς καὶ νόμιμος*** — *Iuramentum, vel impositum et requisitum a Magistratu, vel legitimum, quod fit ex officio vocationis* — An oath, either imposed and required by the Magistrate, or lawful, such as is made by the duty of one's calling.

### *Extrema* · The Extremes

**Contrary to invocation are:**

1. ***τὸ διαλείπειν ἢ ἀποτίθεσθαι τὴν προσευχήν*** — *intermittere, vel omittere precationem* — To leave off, or to lay aside, prayer.
2. ***πᾶσα ψευδὴς προσκύνησις ἐθνική, εἰδωλολατρική, ἰουδαϊκή, ἢ τῶν νεκρῶν*** — *Omnis falsa invocatio, Ethnica, Turcica, Idololatrica, Iudaica, mortuorum* — All false invocation: heathen, Turkish, idolatrous, Jewish, and that of the dead.
3. ***βαττολογίαι*** — *Multiloquia in precatione* — Vain repetitions and much speaking in prayer.
4. ***δεισιδαιμονικαὶ προσευχαί*** — *superstitiosae preces* — Superstitious prayers.

**Contrary to thanksgiving are:**

1. ***ἀχαριστία*** — *ingratitudo* — Unthankfulness.
2. ***ὑποκριτικὴ χάρις*** — *simulatio gratitudinis* — The feigning of thankfulness.

**Contrary to purity of doctrine are:**

1. ***πᾶσα διαφθορὰ τῆς διδαχῆς*** — *Omnis corruptela doctrinae* — Every corruption of doctrine.
2. ***αἵρεσις*** — *Secta, segregatio* — A sect, a setting apart. ***Κεζᾶν***, that is, to separate oneself; whence the German *Ketzer* (heretic).
3. ***πολυπραγμοσύνη ἐν τοῖς περιέργοις καὶ κενοσπούδοις*** — *curiositas in non necessariis materiis* — Curiosity in matters not necessary.
4. ***ἀμέλεια ἐν τῷ διδάσκειν*** — *negligentia in docendo* — Negligence in teaching.
5. ***ἐξάλειψις καὶ ἀφανισμὸς τῆς διδαχῆς*** — *deletio et interitus doctrinae* — The blotting out and perishing of doctrine.

<!-- p.212 -->

**Contrary to confession are:**

1. ***ἀποστασία*** — *defectio* — Falling away.
2. ***ἄρνησις*** — *negatio* — Denial, as that of Peter.
3. ***πανουργία καὶ στροφή*** — *versura et mutabilitas* — Shifting and changeableness, as that also of Ecebolius, ***τοῦ ἀλλοπροσάλλου*** (the turncoat).[^E-2]
4. ***ἰσχυρογνωμοσύνη καὶ ἰδιογνωμοσύνη*** — *pertinacia* — Stubbornness [and self-opinion], as that of Servetus, burned at Geneva in 1552.[^E-3]

**Contrary to the oath are:**

1. ***σοφιστικαὶ καὶ διαβολικαὶ ἐξηγήσεις καὶ ἑρμηνεῖαι τῶν ὅρκων*** — *Sophisticae et calumniosae expositiones iuramentorum* — Sophistical and slanderous expositions of oaths.
2. ***ἀσεβεῖς ὅρκοι*** — *impia iuramenta* — Ungodly oaths.
3. ***βλασφημία*** — *blasphemia* — Blasphemy.
4. ***εὐχαὶ εἰδωλολατρικαί*** — *vota Monachorum, superstitiosae precationes* — The vows of monks, superstitious prayers [idolatrous vows].
5. ***ἐπιορκία*** — *periurium* — Perjury.

## *Virtutes III. Praecepti* · The Virtues of the Third Commandment

### *Officia docentium* · The Duties of Them That Teach

1. ***τὸ πιστὸν τῶν διδασκάλων*** — *Fidelitas docentium* — The faithfulness of them that teach.
2. ***γνῶσις τῆς ἀληθοῦς διδαχῆς*** — *Notitia verae doctrinae* — The knowledge of true doctrine.
3. ***ἐπιμονή*** — *Constantia* — Constancy.
4. ***ἐπιμέλεια*** — *diligentia in sex partibus* ***τῆς διακονίας*** *sive ministerii consistens, quae sunt* — Diligence, which consisteth in six parts of the ministry, which are:
    1. ***τὰ ἀληθῆ διδάσκειν καὶ διαλέγεσθαι*** — *Vera praedicatio* — True preaching [to teach and to discourse of the things that are true].
    2. ***θρησκεία ἀληθὴς καὶ ὁσίη τῶν μυστηρίων*** — *administratio vera sacramentorum* — The true [and holy] administration of the sacraments.
    3. ***ἀναγγέλλειν τὴν τῶν ἁμαρτιῶν ἄφεσιν, καὶ ἀπολύειν τοὺς μετανοοῦντας*** — *annuntiare remissionem peccatorum, et absolvere poenitentes* — To declare the remission of sins, and to absolve the penitent.
    4. ***ἐπισκέπτεσθαι καὶ παραμυθεῖσθαι τοὺς νοσερούς, καὶ ἄλλους κακῶς ἔχοντας*** — *visitare et consolari aegrotos et alios male habentes* — To visit and to comfort the sick, and others that are in evil case.
    5. <!-- p.213 -->***ἀπαυδᾶν, ἀφορίζειν, ἐκκηρύττειν τοὺς δυσάρκτους καὶ ἀπειθεῖς*** — *excommunicare contumaces* — To excommunicate the contumacious [to forbid, to separate, to proclaim as cast out the unruly and disobedient].
    6. ***παραδείγματα καλῶν ἔργων παρέχειν*** — *Exemplis virtutum praelucere* — To shine before others with examples of the virtues.
5. ***ὁμόνοια σὺν τοῖς συνέδροις καὶ συνδούλοις*** — *Concordia cum collegis* — Concord with colleagues [with them that sit with us and serve with us].

**Contrary to these duties of them that teach are:**

1. ***ἡ ἄγνοια τῆς διδαχῆς*** — *ignoratio doctrinae* — Ignorance of the doctrine.
2. ***ἀπιστία*** — *infidelitas* — Unfaithfulness.
3. ***ἀμέλεια*** — *negligentia* — Negligence.
4. ***μικροψυχία*** — *pusillanimitas* — Faintheartedness.
5. ***περιπέτεια καὶ ἀβουλία*** — *temeritas, et inconsideratio* — Rashness and inconsiderateness.[^E-4]
6. ***ἐλαφρία καὶ κουφότης*** — *levitas* — Lightness.
7. ***ἀστασία*** — *inconstantia* — Inconstancy.
8. ***τὸ πρόσκομμα γενέσθαι τινί*** — *aliis esse malo exemplo, et offensioni* — To be an evil example and a stumbling-block to others.
9. ***διχοστασία, καὶ φιλονεικία*** — *discordia et studium contentionum* — Dissension and the love of contentions.

### *Officia auditorum* · The Duties of Hearers

***τὸ τῶν ἀκουόντων ἢ ἀκροωμένων καθῆκον*** (the duty of them that hear, or that give ear).

1. ***ἁγιασμὸς τοῦ σαββάτου*** — *sanctificatio sabbati* — The sanctifying of the Sabbath; that is, ***ἡ σπουδὴ καὶ ἐπιμέλεια ἐν τῷ γινώσκειν, ἀκούειν, μανθάνειν, καὶ ὁμολογεῖν*** — *diligentia in cognoscendo, audiendo, discendo et confitendo* — diligence in knowing, hearing, learning and confessing.
2. ***αἰδὼς πρὸς τοὺς διακόνους*** — *reverentia erga ministros* — Reverence toward the ministers. **2 Cor. 5.** **Eph. 4.**
3. ***εὐπείθεια τῶν πρὸς διδαχήν*** — *Obedientia in iis, quae ad ministerium pertinent, ubi verbum Dei postulat obedientiam* — Obedience in those things which pertain to the ministry, where the Word of God requireth obedience.
4. ***ἐπιείκεια*** — *Aequitas in iudicandis docentium moribus* — Equity in judging the manners of them that teach.
5. ***τὸ κατατίθεσθαι καὶ συμβάλλειν τι κατὰ τὰ ὑπάρχοντα, πρὸς τροφὴν καὶ ἱκανὰ τῶν διδασκόντων*** — *collatio facultatum ad alendos docentes* — The contributing of one's substance for the maintenance of them that teach. **1 Cor. 6.**
6. ***ἡ εὐσεβὴς παρατήρησις τῶν ἱερομηνίων τῶν θεοεπέων, τουτέστι, φερομένων ἀπὸ τοῦ θεοῦ, καὶ τῶν τὴν πολιτείαν, εὐταξίαν, καὶ*** <!-- p.214 -->***ἐγκράτειαν σωζουσῶν*** — *Pia observatio ceremoniarum, et quae a Deo sunt mandatae, et quae bonum ordinem et disciplinam servant* — The godly observance of ceremonies, both of those which are commanded of God, and of those which preserve good order and discipline.

### *Extrema* · The Extremes

**The extremes contrary to the virtues which in the Third Commandment are required of hearers are these:**

1. ***ἡ βεβήλωσις τοῦ σαββάτου*** — *profanatio sabbati* — The profaning of the Sabbath.
2. ***καταφρόνησις τῶν ἐκκλησιαστικῶν πανηγυρῶν ἢ συνάξεων, καὶ τῆς εὐαγγελικῆς πολιτείας, καὶ τοῦ κηρύγματος*** — *contemtus conventuum, festorum, et concionum in Ecclesia* — Contempt of the assemblies, feasts and sermons in the Church.
3. ***τῶν μυστηρίων ἤτοι μὴ μεταλαβεῖν σπουδαίως, ἢ μετέχειν ἀναξίως, ἢ καὶ ὑβριστικῶς ἀπέχεσθαι*** — *Sacramentis non uti, vel indigne uti, vel ea contumaciter negligere* — Not to use the Sacraments, or to use them unworthily, or contumaciously to neglect them.
4. ***τὰς ἑορτασίμους τῶν ἡμερῶν μετενεγκεῖν εἰς ἄλλας τινὰς ἀσχολίας, δι' ὧν ἐμποδίζοιτ' ἂν ὁ ἁγιασμός*** — *Festos dies transferre ad alias occupationes, delitias, voluptates, quibus impeditur sanctificatio* — To turn the feast days to other occupations, delights and pleasures, whereby the sanctifying of them is hindered.
5. ***τὰς λειτουργήσεις μεταστρέφειν πρὸς πλεονεξίαν, τόκον, πορισμόν, κέρδος ἢ ἐργασίαν*** — *abuti iis, quae ad publicum ministerium pertinent, ad avaritiam, usuram, quaestum, lucrum* — To abuse the things that pertain to the public ministry unto covetousness, usury, gain and lucre.
6. ***τοὺς πιστοὺς τοῦ λόγου κήρυκας, ποιμένας, καὶ διδασκάλους μὴ ὠφελεῖν*** — *non iuvare fideles Pastores et doctores* — Not to help faithful Pastors and teachers.
7. ***δολοφροσύνη ὑποκριτική, ἡ ἐχθρεύουσα τῇ τοῦ θείου λόγου διακονίᾳ*** — *simulatio arte premens ministerium* — A dissembling that by craft oppresseth the ministry.
8. ***προσόδους, ἢ χρήματα καὶ κτήματα τὰ ἐκκλησιαστικὰ μηχανᾶσθαι, καὶ ἁρπάζειν*** — *rapere bona ecclesiastica* — To seize upon the goods of the Church, as do our Evangelical Princes and nobles.
9. ***καταναλόειν τὰ χρήματα εἰς εἰδωλολατρείαν*** — *profundere pecunias ad idololatriam, Missam, monasteria* — To pour out money upon idolatry, the Mass, monasteries.
10. ***δεισιδαιμονία*** — *superstitio* — Superstition.

## *Virtutes IIII. Praecepti* · The Virtues of the Fourth Commandment

1. ***φιλοστοργία καὶ εὐσέβεια πρὸς τοὺς γονεῖς, καὶ τὰ τέκνα, καὶ τοὺς ἄλλους συγγενεῖς*** — *Pietas erga parentes, natos, et sanguine iunctos* — Dutiful affection toward parents, children, and them that are joined by blood.
2. ***δικαιοσύνη καθ' ὅλου, ἡ συλλήβδην πάσας ἀρετὰς ἔχουσα*** <!-- p.215 -->— *Iustitia universalis, id est, obedientia erga omnes Leges, honestas in societate politica* — Universal justice [which comprehendeth all the virtues together], that is, obedience toward all the Laws, honesty in civil society.
3. ***δικαιοσύνη διανεμητική*** — *Iustitia distributiva, quae fit proportione geometrica* — Distributive justice, which is done by geometrical proportion.
4. ***ἐπιμέλεια καὶ ἐνδελέχεια ἐν τοῖς πράγμασι*** — *Sedulitas* — Sedulity [diligence and continuance in one's business].
5. ***εὐχαριστία· ἀντίδωρον· θρεπτήριον· ἢ τὸ ἀντιπελαργεῖν*** — *Gratitudo* — Gratitude [thankfulness; a gift in return; the recompense of nurture; or to cherish in turn, as the stork doth].
6. ***αἰδώς*** — *Reverentia* — Reverence.
7. ***σεμνότης*** — *Gravitas* — Gravity.
8. ***μετριότης καὶ σωφροσύνη*** — *modestia* — Modesty.
9. ***φιλησυχία*** — *pacis et tranquillitatis studium in officiis vocationis* — The love of peace and quietness in the duties of one's calling.
10. ***φιλοκαλία*** — *honestatis studium, et gloriae appetitio* — The love of honesty, and the desire of good report. ***εὔκλεια*** (good fame).
11. ***ἡ ἀγαθὴ συνείδησις*** — *bona conscientia* — A good conscience.

### *Extrema* · The Extremes

**Contrary to dutiful affection toward our own are:**

1. ***ἀστοργία*** — *carere naturali amore* — To be without natural affection.
2. ***ἀκηδία καὶ ἔνδοσις*** — *indulgentia* — Indulgence [carelessness and giving way].
3. ***ἀσέβεια τῶν παίδων*** — *liberorum impietas* — The undutifulness of children, as of Tullia.[^E-5]
4. ***ἀσεβὴς καὶ δεισιδαιμονικὴ ὑποταγὴ ἢ ὑπακοή*** — *impia et superstitiosa obedientia, seu servitus* — Ungodly and superstitious obedience, or servitude.
5. ***στάσις*** — *seditio* — Sedition.

**Contrary to universal justice are:**

1. ***ἀπείθεια καὶ αὐθάδεια ἢ προπέτεια*** — *contumacia* — Contumacy.
2. ***ἀδικία*** — *iniustitia* — Injustice.
3. ***στάσις*** — *seditio* — Sedition, and all ***παρανομία καὶ ἀνομία*** — *legum transgressio* — transgression of the laws.
4. ***ὑποταγὴ ἀσεβής*** — *impia obedientia* — Ungodly obedience.

**Contrary to distributive justice are:**

1. ***σύγχυσις τῶν τάξεων ἢ συστημάτων ἐν τῷ βίῳ, ἥ ἐστιν ἀναρχία*** — *confusio graduum* — The confounding of degrees [of the orders or estates in life, which is anarchy].
2. ***ἰσότης ἡ ἀριθμητική*** — *aequalitas Arithmetica* — Arithmetical equality.
3. ***τυραννὶς καὶ ὠμότης*** — *crudelitas* — Cruelty [tyranny and savageness].
4. ***ἔνδοσις*** — *indulgentia et remissio* — Indulgence and slackness.

**Contrary to sedulity are:**

1. ***ῥᾳθυμία καὶ ἀργία*** — *ignavia* — Sloth.
2. ***πολυπραγμοσύνη*** — *aliena dicere, et facere* — To say and to do the things that belong to others.

**Contrary to gratitude are:**

1. ***ἀχαριστία*** — *ingratitudo* — Ingratitude.
2. ***ὑπόκρισις*** — *simulatio gratitudinis* — The feigning of gratitude.
3. ***κολακεία*** — *adulatio* — Flattery.

<!-- p.216 -->

**Contrary to reverence are:**

1. ***ἀλαζονεία*** — *superbia, contemnens alios* — Pride, despising others.
2. ***ζήλωσις, καὶ κολακεία*** — *affectatio et assentatio* — Affectation and fawning.

**Contrary to gravity are:**

1. ***κουφότης*** — *levitas* — Lightness; and ***βωμολοχία*** — *scurrilitas, a* ***σκώς***, *id est stercus* — scurrility, from ***σκώς***, that is, dung.
2. ***ἀγροικία καὶ σκαιότης*** — *rusticitas* — Clownishness.
3. ***δυσχέρεια*** — *morositas et difficultas* — Moroseness and frowardness.
4. ***εἰρωνεία*** — *irrisio, dissimulatio* — Mockery, dissembling.

**Contrary to modesty:**

1. ***ἀλαζονεία*** — *iactantia, superbia* — Boasting, pride.
2. ***ἐλαφρία*** — *levitas* — Lightness; and ***γελωτοποιΐα*** — *scurrilitas* — buffoonery.
3. ***αἰσχρότης*** — *obscoenitas* — Filthiness.
4. ***φιλοτιμία*** — *ambitio* — Ambition.
5. ***ζῆλος, ἔρις*** — *aemulatio, contentio* — Emulation, strife.
6. ***ἀναισχυντία*** — *impudentia* — Shamelessness. ***ἄτρεπτον εἶναι πρὸς τὸ κακῶς ἀκούειν, καὶ ἀπαθῆ ὄντα ὀλιγωρίᾳ δόξης*** (to be unmoved at being evil spoken of, and without feeling, through contempt of good report).
7. ***ἔκπληξις*** — *stupor* — Dullness.
8. ***ὑπόκρισις*** — *simulatio modestiae* — The feigning of modesty.

**Contrary to the love of quietness, to the desire of good report, and to the care of a good conscience** are the same vices; chiefly contention, ***πολυπραγμοσύνη*** (meddlesomeness), shamelessness, ambition, and the like.

## *Virtutes V. Praecepti* · The Virtues of the Fifth Commandment

1. ***ἡ ἐν μέρει ἢ κατὰ μέρος δικαιοσύνη*** — *iustitia particularis, tribuens unicuique, quod suum est* — Particular justice, which rendereth to every man that which is his own.
2. ***δικαιοσύνη διανεμητικὴ ἐν ταῖς τιμωρίαις*** — *distributiva iustitia, afficiens sceleratos poenis* — Distributive justice, which visiteth evildoers with punishments.
3. ***ἀνδρεία*** (fortitude), either:
    - ***τηβενοφόρος*** — *fortitudo togata* — the fortitude of the gown, ***εἰρηνική*** (of peace); or
    - ***πολεμικὴ ὑπὲρ βωμῶν καὶ ἑστίας*** — *fortitudo bellica pro lege et pro grege, sive pro Aris et focis* — the fortitude of war, for the law and for the flock, or for altars and hearths.
4. ***ὑπομονὴ φιλοσοφικὴ ἡ στέργουσα τὰ παρόντα*** — *patientia philosophica, boni consulens praesentia* — Philosophical patience, which taketh in good part the things that are present, according to the saying: ***ἀνέχου καὶ ἀπέχου***, *sustine et abstine*, bear and forbear, *leid und meid*.[^E-6]
5. ***πραότης*** — *mansuetudo* — Meekness.
6. ***ἐπιείκεια*** — *aequitas* — Equity. ***χρηστότης*** — *lenitas* — Gentleness.
7. ***τὸ ἔλεος, ἢ ἡ ἐλεημοσύνη*** — *misericordia* — Mercy.
8. ***φιλησυχία*** — *studium concordiae* — The love of concord. ***φιλοφιλία*** (the love of friends).
9. <!-- p.217 -->***φιλία*** — *amicitia* — Friendship, which is made either:
    - ***φύσει*** — *natura* — by nature: men with God; husband and wife, ***σύζυγοι***, ***ἀρχὴ γαμική*** (the rule of wedlock); parents and children, ***τοκεῖς, τέκνα***; kinsfolk, ***συγγενεῖς***;
    - ***νόμῳ*** — *lege* — by law, as: ***πατρίς***, *patria*, one's country; ***ἐκκλησία***, the Church; ***πολιτεία***, *respublica*, the commonwealth; ***οἱ κατὰ τὴν οἰκίαν***, *familia*, the household;
    - ***χρήσει*** — *usu* — by use, as: ***γείτονες***, *vicini*, neighbours; ***ὁμοσίπυοι, ὁμόκαπνοι, σύσσιτοι, ὁμοῤῥόφιοι***, *contubernales, convictores*, them that share one lodging and one table [one meal-tub, one smoke, one board, one roof]; ***συνοδοίποροι, ὀπαδοί, ἀκόλουθοι***, *comites*, companions [fellow-travellers, attendants, followers]; ***συνάρχοντες, παραστάται***, *collegae, manipulares*, colleagues, comrades in the ranks.
10. ***τὸ πιστόν*** — *fidelitas* — Faithfulness.
11. ***εὐγνωμοσύνη*** — *candor animi* — Candour of mind.
12. ***φιλανθρωπία*** — *humanitas* — Humanity.
13. ***εὐπροσηγορία*** — *affabilitas* — Affability.
14. ***Νέμεσις ἢ ζῆλος*** — *zelus* — Zeal.

### *Extrema* · The Extremes

**Contrary to particular justice are:**

1. ***ὕβρις καὶ ἀδικία*** — *iniuria, violentia* — Wrong, violence.
2. ***τὸ λίαν δίκαιον, ἢ ἀκριβοδίκαιον*** — *Summum ius* — The utmost rigour of right.
3. ***διαβολή*** — *fraus et calumnia* — Fraud and slander.
4. ***ἀσέλγεια*** — *petulantia* — Wantonness.
5. ***μοχθηρία, ἢ πανουργία*** — *nequitia* — Wickedness [or knavery].

**Contrary to distributive justice in the execution of punishments are:**

1. ***ἔνδοσις καὶ ἐπίμυσις*** — *indulgentia et conniventia* — Indulgence and connivance.
2. ***προσωποληψία*** — *inaequalitas, respectus personarum* — Inequality, respect of persons, according to the verse:

    > *Dat veniam corvis, vexat censura columbas.*  
    > Censure giveth pardon to the ravens, and vexeth the doves.[^E-7]

3. ***ἀπηνότης καὶ ὠμότης*** — *crudelitas* — Cruelty.

<!-- p.218 -->

**Contrary to the fortitude of the gown are:**

1. ***κουφότης καὶ ἀστασία*** — *levitas et inconstantia* — Lightness and inconstancy.
2. ***μαλακία*** — *mollities animi* — Softness of mind.
3. ***ἰσχυρογνωμοσύνη*** — *pervicacia, pertinacia* — Obstinacy, stubbornness.
4. ***δυσχέρεια ἡ στωϊκή*** — *morositas Stoica* — Stoical moroseness, that is, ***τὸ εἶναι δύσκολον καὶ σκυθρωπόν*** (to be froward and of a sour countenance).

**But contrary to the fortitude of war are:**

1. ***δειλότης*** — *timiditas* — Cowardice.
2. ***αἰσχρουργία*** — *foedum facinus* — A foul deed, as drunkenness, whoredom, blasphemy, unjust manslaughter, rapine, shameful flight, ***καὶ τὸ εἶναι ῥίψασπιδα*** (and to be one that casteth away his shield), as the soldiers are wont to do today.
3. ***περιπέτεια, καὶ ἀβουλία*** — *temeritas* — Rashness.

**Contrary to philosophical patience are:**

1. ***τῶν παρόντων καὶ εἰωθότων ὑπεροψία, ἢ τὸ ἁψίκορον*** — *fastidium rerum praesentium* — A loathing of things present [a disdain of things present and accustomed, or the being soon sated].
2. ***ἀτλησία*** — *impatientia* — Impatience.
3. ***ἀσυνεσία καὶ ῥᾳθυμία ῥᾳστωνεύουσα*** — *socordia et ignavia negligens occasiones, quibus offeruntur bona* — Dullness and sloth, neglecting the occasions whereby good things are offered.
4. ***κατάπληξις*** — *stupor* — Amazement.

**Contrary to meekness are:**

1. ***ὀργιλότης*** — *iracundia* — Wrathfulness.
2. ***τὸ μῖσος*** — *odium* — Hatred.
3. ***χαυνότης*** — *lentitas* — Slackness.

**Contrary to equity:**

1. ***κουφότης*** — *levitas* — Lightness.
2. ***ἔνδοσις*** — *indulgentia* — Indulgence, or ***ἄνεσις κακή*** — *remissio, et venia immodica* — slackness and immoderate pardon.
3. ***ἀπήνεια καὶ ἀπανθρωπία*** — *crudelitas* — Cruelty.
4. ***ἐκδίκησις καὶ ἐναντίωσις ἀμυντική*** — *vindicta privata* — Private revenge.

**Contrary to mercy:**

1. ***συγγνώμη βλαβερή*** — *indulgentia* — Indulgence [a hurtful pardoning].
2. ***ὠμότης*** — *crudelitas* — Cruelty.
3. ***ἐπιχαιρεκακία*** — *laetari in aliorum aerumnis et adversitatibus* — To rejoice in the miseries and adversities of others.

**Contrary to the love of concord:**

1. ***φιλονεικία*** — *serens discordias* — Contentiousness, sowing discords.
2. ***φθόνος*** — *invidentia, livor* — Envy, spite.
3. ***βραδυτὴς καὶ χαυνότης ἐν τῇ τῆς ἀληθείας ὁμολογίᾳ*** — *lentitas non pugnans de rebus necessariis* — A slackness [in the confession of the truth] that contendeth not for things necessary, such as there is today in most Princes and Theologians, who, under the colour and pretext of concord, let go and lose the truth.

**Contrary to friendship are:**

1. ***ἔχθρα*** — *inimicitia* — Enmity.
2. ***τὸ μῖσος*** — *odium* — Hatred.
3. ***ὑπόκρισις*** — *simulatio amicitiae* — The feigning of friendship.
4. ***μισανθρωπία*** — *inhumanitas* — Inhumanity.
5. ***ἀρέσκεια ἢ κολακεία*** — *comitas assentatrix, seu* <!-- p.219 -->*adulatio Aulica* — A fawning complaisance, or the flattery of Courts. ***κακαὶ φρένες, ἡδὺ λάλημμα*** — *fel in corde, mel in ore* — gall in the heart, honey in the mouth. ***τὸ φίλημα ἢ στόμιον τοῦ Ἰούδα*** — *osculum Iudae* — the kiss of Judas. Cadmean brethren,[^E-8] such as many are today, even among the Theologians.
6. ***τὸ συγκατανευσίφαγον εἶναι*** — *esse conepulonem, et parasitum, et omnium dictis inter epulas facile annuere* — To be a fellow-feaster and a parasite, and readily to nod assent at table to whatsoever any man saith.

**Contrary to faithfulness:**

1. ***ἀπιστία*** — *perfidia* — Perfidy.
2. ***κολακεία*** — *assentatio, et stulta fidelitas contra bonos mores* — Fawning, and a foolish faithfulness against good manners.

**Contrary to candour of mind:**

1. ***ὑποψία καὶ ὑφόρασις*** — *suspicio* — Suspicion.
2. ***ἀπειθία ἢ δυσπειθία*** — *diffidentia* — Distrust.
3. ***ἑτοιμοπιστία, ταχυπείθεια*** — *nimia credulitas* — Overmuch credulity.[^E-9]
4. ***φθόνος*** — *invidia* — Envy.
5. ***ἐπιχαιρεκακία*** (joy at another's hurt).
6. ***ὑπόκρισις*** (dissembling).
7. ***λυκοφιλία, ὑαινοφιλία*** (the friendship of the wolf, the friendship of the hyena).

**Contrary to humanity:**

1. ***δυσχέρεια*** — *morositas* — Moroseness. ***μισανθρωπία*** (the hatred of mankind).
2. ***ἀστασία ἢ ὑπόκρισις*** — *levitas et simulatio* — Lightness and dissembling, such as there is in Courts.

**Contrary to affability** is to be ***σκυθρωπόν***, *morosum, torvum*, sour and grim, ***δυσπρόσοδον, δυσόμιλον καὶ δυσέντευκτον***, *aditu difficilem et inaffabilem*, hard of access and not to be spoken with.

**Contrary to zeal:**

1. ***χαύνωσις*** — *lentitas et laxatio* — Slackness and remissness.
2. ***ἀγριότης*** — *feritas, et iniusta asperitas* — Fierceness and unjust harshness.

## *Virtutes VI. Praecepti* · The Virtues of the Sixth Commandment

**I.**

**The virtue:**

- ***ἁγνεία ἢ καθαρότης*** — *Castitas* — Chastity.
- ***παρθενία*** — *Virginitas* — Virginity.
- ***γάμος*** — *Nuptiae* — Marriage.
- ***συζυγία*** — *coniugium* — Wedlock.

**In defect:**

- ***ἐπιθυμία*** — *libido* — Lust.
- ***πορνεία*** — *scortatio* — Whoredom.
- ***μοιχεία*** — *adulterium* — Adultery.
- ***τὸ ἀπολύειν τὴν γυναῖκα*** — *repudiare uxorem* — To put away one's wife.
- ***διακόρευσις*** — *stuprum* — Defloration.
- ***φθορὰ καὶ σύζευξις τῶν*** <!-- p.220 -->***συγγενῶν*** — *incestus* — Incest.
- ***λογισμοὶ σαρκικοί*** — *cogitationes spurcae* — Filthy thoughts.
- ***αἰσχρολογίαι*** — *obscoeni sermones* — Filthy communications.
- ***πᾶσα ἀκαθαρσία, καὶ τῶν ἀφροδισίων χρῆσις ἄνομος καὶ ἄθεσμος*** — *omnis impuritas et incontinentia* — All uncleanness and incontinence.

**In excess:**

- ***ζυγὸν παρθενίας κατ' ἀνάγκην*** — *prohibitiones coniugii* — The forbiddings of marriage [a yoke of virginity by constraint]. **1 Tim. 4.**
- ***ἀγαμία βίαιος*** — *coelibatus violentus* — Enforced celibacy.
- ***ὑποκριτικὴ ἁγνεία*** — *simulata castitas* — Feigned chastity, as that of Origen.

**II.**

**The virtue:** ***ἡ αἰδὼς καὶ σωφροσύνη*** — *verecundia sive pudor* — Shamefacedness, or modesty.

**In defect:**

- ***ἀναίδεια*** — *impudentia* — Shamelessness.
- ***ἀκολασία καὶ ἀτασθαλία*** — *protervitas et contumelia* — Wantonness and insolence.
- ***ματαιότης*** — *vanitas* — Vanity.

**In excess:** ***δυσωπία*** — *stupor rusticus* — A clownish bashfulness.

**III.**

**The virtue:** ***σωφροσύνη*** — *Temperantia* — Temperance. ***νηφαλισμὸς καὶ προσοχή*** — *sobrietas* — Sobriety.

**In defect:**

- ***ἀκρασία*** — *intemperantia* — Intemperance.
- ***ἀσωτία*** — *luxuria* — Riotous living, of which it is said: Every ***ἄσωτος*** is ***ἄσωστος*** (every prodigal is past saving).
- ***μέθη*** — *ebrietas* — Drunkenness.
- ***πολυφαγία*** — *voracitas* — Voracity.
- ***λαιμαργία*** — *gula* — Gluttony. *Crapula* (surfeiting), from ***κάρα πάλλειν*** (to make the head to shake). *Eluatio* (gormandizing), ***λαιμαργία***. ***ἀσωτοποσία*** (riotous drinking).

**In excess:**

- ***νηστεία ἡ ὑπερμέτρως*** — *ieiunium immodicum* — Immoderate fasting.
- ***ἰχθυοφαγία δεισιδαιμονική*** — *superstitiosus piscium esus* — The superstitious eating of fish.
- ***ἐγκράτεια βίαιος*** — *abstinentia nimia et violenta* — Excessive and enforced abstinence.

## *Virtutes VII. Praecepti* · The Virtues of the Seventh Commandment

**I.**

**The virtue:** ***τὰ ἀναγκαῖα*** <!-- p.221 -->***κτᾶσθαι ἐννόμως, καὶ τοῖς ἰδίοις κτήμασι χρῆσθαι ἐλευθερίως*** — *Iusta possessio et bonus usus rerum* — The just possession and the good use of things [to get things needful lawfully, and to use one's own possessions liberally].

**In defect:**

- ***πᾶσαι ἀφαιρέσεις τῶν ἀλλοτρίων*** — *omnia aucupia opum* — All fowling after wealth [all takings away of other men's goods].
- ***κλοπαί*** — *furta* — Thefts.
- ***δόλοι ἐν τοῖς συναλλάγμασι*** — *fraudes in contractibus* — Frauds in contracts.

**In excess:** ***πλεονεξία*** — *avaritia* — Covetousness.

**II.**

**The virtue:** ***δικαιοσύνη ἀλλακτική*** — *Iustitia commutativa constans* ***ἰσότητι*** *iuxta proportionem Arithmeticam* — Commutative justice, which standeth in equality, according to arithmetical proportion.

**In defect:**

- ***συνάλλαγμα ἄδικον*** — *iniustus contractus* — An unjust contract.
- ***πανούργευμα, καὶ χρηματισμὸς ἄμετρος καὶ ἄδικος*** — *lucrum nimium et iniustum, supra sortem* — Excessive and unjust gain, above the principal.
- ***κέρδος ἄδικον*** — *lucrum iniustum* — Unjust lucre.
- ***ἁρπαγὴ καὶ σύλησις*** — *Expilatio publica* — Public pillage, ***οἷά ἐστι, τὰ ὑπὸ τῶν δυνατῶν καὶ ἀρχόντων ἐπιτιθέμενα τοῖς πελάταις καὶ ἀρχομένοις τῶν εἰσπράξεων καὶ φόρων καὶ τελέων ἄχθη, καὶ ἤτοι πεπανουργευμέναι ἢ βεβιασμέναι τῶν χρημάτων ἀποτρίψεις, καὶ συσκευαί*** (such as are the burdens of exactions and tributes and tolls laid by the mighty and by rulers upon their dependants and subjects, and the wringings away of money, whether by craft or by force, and the contrivances), the arts of publicans and treasurers.
- Likewise, ***βία τῶν ἀλλοτρίων ἁρπαζομένων*** — *vis bonorum raptorum* — The violent seizing of other men's goods.
- ***κλοπαὶ αἱ ἴδιαι ἢ τῶν ἰδίων*** — *furta privata* — Private thefts, of which a description is found in the notable theft in **Herodotus**.[^E-10]
- Likewise, ***ἀποφορὰ καὶ μισθὸς τῆς παρεργούσης ἀπάτης καὶ ἀργίας ἢ ῥᾳθυμίας*** — *iniustae mercedes et stipendia, quae devorantur ab ignavis et deceptoribus* — Unjust wages and stipends, which are devoured by idlers and deceivers, in whom most princes today take delight, to the great hurt and dishonour both of themselves and of their subjects.
- And hither let there be referred also ***ἁρπαγμοί, καὶ αἰφνίδιοι καὶ μετὰ βίας ἀφαιρέσεις τῶν ἀγαθῶν τῶν ἐκκλησιαστικῶν*** — *rapinae bonorum Ecclesiasticorum* — the robberies of the goods of the Church, which are enjoyed chiefly by ***τὰ ἐτώσια*** <!-- p.222 -->***ἄχθη ἀρούρης, καὶ κακὰ θηρία, γαστέρες ἀργαί*** (the useless burdens of the earth, and evil beasts, slow bellies).[^E-11]
- Moreover also ***ἡ ῥᾳθυμία, ἀπιστία, καὶ ἀργία τῶν ὑπηρετῶν τῶν κατ' οἰκίαν*** — *ignavia et infidelitas familiae* — The sloth and unfaithfulness of the household.
- Likewise ***πτωχεία τῶν ἀέργων καὶ κακούργων*** — *mendicitas ignavorum, et fraudulentorum hominum* — The beggary of idle and deceitful men.

**In excess:**

- ***ἡ ἀσωτία*** — *prodigentia et profusio* — Prodigality and lavishing.
- ***τὸ σφαῖραν ἀποδεικνύναι τὴν οὐσίαν*** (to make a ball of one's substance).

**III.**

**The virtue:** ***Ἐλευθεριότης καὶ εὐποιΐα ἐκ τῶν ὑπαρχόντων*** — *Liberalitas et beneficentia* — Liberality and beneficence [out of that which one hath].

**In defect:** ***ἀνελευθερία. βαναυσία. πλεονεξία. ῥυπαρία. μικροπρέπεια. σμικρολογία.*** — *avaritia et sorditudo, nemini benefaciens* — Covetousness and sordidness, doing good to no man [illiberality; vulgar meanness; greed; filthy niggardliness; pettiness; penny-counting].

**In excess:** ***πρόχευμα καὶ ἀσωτία*** — *profusio, et prodigalitas* — Lavishing and prodigality.

**IIII.**

**The virtue:** ***φειδὼ καὶ πρόνοια*** — *Parsimonia, frugalitas* — Thrift, frugality [and forethought]; ***αὐτάρκεια καὶ σωφροσύνη*** (contentment and soberness).

**In defect:** ***τὸ σπαθᾶν καὶ τρυφᾶν*** — *prodigalitas* — Prodigality [to squander and to live delicately].

**In excess:**

- ***ἀδικία καὶ βία*** — *iniustitia et vis* — Injustice and violence.
- ***φειδωλία*** — *tenacitas et avaritia* — Closefistedness and covetousness.

**V.**

**The virtue:** ***φιλοξενία*** — *Hospitalitas* — Hospitality. ***ξενισμός, καὶ ξένισις*** (the entertaining of strangers).

**In defect:** ***ἀξενία*** — *inhospitalitas* — Inhospitality, joined with ***μισανθρωπίᾳ καὶ πλεονεξίᾳ***, *inhumanitate et avaritia*, inhumanity and covetousness. It is called <!-- p.223 -->also ***ἀμιξία*** (unsociableness), and ***κακοξενία*** (evil entertainment of strangers).

**In excess:**

- ***τὸ ἀλαζονεύεσθαι καὶ σοβαρεύεσθαι*** — *ostentatio et fastus* — Ostentation and haughtiness.
- ***ἀσωτία*** — *profusio* — Lavishing.
- ***τὸ ξενίζειν πάντας ἀκρίτως*** — *Omnes sine discrimine hospitio excipere* — To receive all men to lodging without distinction, which is the part of lightness, and of innkeepers.

## *Virtutes VIII. Praecepti* · The Virtues of the Eighth Commandment

**I.**

***Ἀλήθεια*** — *Veritas* — Truth: ***ἡ πάντων μὲν ἀγαθῶν θεῷ ἡγεῖται, πάντων δὲ ἀνθρώποις*** (which is the leader of all good things for God, and of all for men).[^E-12]

**Contrary to truth are:**

1. ***πᾶν ψεῦδος ἐν τῷ βίῳ, ἢ ψευδολογία, καὶ πᾶσα σοφιστεία καὶ βεβιασμένη ἐξήγησις περὶ τὰ μαθήματα*** — *Omne mendacium in vita et oratione, et omnis sophistica in disciplinis* — Every lie in life and in speech, and all sophistry [and forced exposition] in the sciences.
2. ***αἵρεσις*** — *haeresis* — Heresy, ***ἡ δόξα βιαζομένη τὴν ἀλήθειαν*** (the opinion that doth violence to the truth); ***λεπτολογίαι, ἢ λεπτολεσχίαι*** — *subtilia nugamenta* — subtle triflings, such as are today those of the Sacramentarians; ***λεπτομέρεια*** — *exilitas* — thin-spun slightness.
3. ***πᾶσα διαβολή*** — *omnis calumnia* — All slander; ***ἡ λαθραίως κατηγορία ἐξ ἐρημίας γενομένη, τὸν κατηγορούμενον λεληθυῖα, ἐκ τοῦ μονομεροῦς ἀναντιλέκτως πεπιστευμένη*** — *clandestina delatio, accusato absente et inscio, et altera tantum parte audita, contradicente nemine* — secret informing, the accused being absent and unaware, and one side only being heard, no man gainsaying.
4. ***ἀπιστία ἐν ταῖς ὑποσχέσεσιν, ἐπαγγελίαις, ὅρκοις, συναλλάγμασι, σπονδαῖς*** — *perfidia in promissionibus, iuramentis, contractibus et foederibus* — Faithlessness in promises, oaths, contracts and leagues.
5. ***εἰρωνεῖαι*** — *irrisiones, et dissimulationes* — Mockings and dissemblings, as are sarcasm, *mycterismus* (the sneer), and ***ἀστεϊσμός*** (the polished jest). Likewise, ***σκώμματα τῆς ὑποκρίσεως ἐνεδρευτικὰ τῶν πλησίον*** — *scommata, quibus insidiae proximo tacite struuntur* — gibes whereby snares are privily laid for one's neighbour.
6. ***συκοφαντία, ἐπηρεασμός, μεταλαλία[?], ἀλαζονεία, κολακεία, ἀστασία, μηδὲν ὑγιές*** — *sycophantia, iactantia, obtrectatio, fastus, adulatio, levitas, nihil sinceri* — False accusing, vaunting, backbiting, haughtiness, flattery, lightness, nothing sound.[^E-13] ***προπέτεια*** — *immodestia* — Forwardness. ***γλῶσσα*** <!-- p.224 -->***ἀκόλαστος*** — *petulans lingua* — An unbridled tongue, ***ἧς τέλος ἡ δυστυχία*** (whose end is misfortune).[^E-14] Likewise ***αἰσχρολογία*** — *turpitudo in sermone* — filthiness in speech.
7. ***πᾶσα ὑπόκρισις ἐν τοῖς λόγοις, ἤθεσι, τρόποις, καὶ ἐν τῷ ὅλῳ βίῳ*** — *simulatio in sermonibus, moribus, gestibus, et in tota vita* — All dissembling in words, manners, bearing, and in the whole life.
8. ***κατάκρυψις μετ' ἐπιβουλῆς, ἢ τὸ ὕπουλον*** — *occultatio insidiosa* — Treacherous concealment [or that which festereth beneath the surface].

**II.**

**The virtue:** ***ἁπλότης*** — *Simplicitas* — Simplicity. ***τὸ σαφές*** — *perspicuitas* — Plainness.

**In defect:**

- ***διπλότης καὶ φενακισμός*** — *duplicitas et impostura* — Doubleness and imposture.
- ***τὸ σκότισον***, of **Heraclitus**: *obscurus esto*, "Be thou dark." ***τὸ ἀκατάληπτον*** (that which cannot be comprehended).

**In excess:**

- ***ὑπόκρισις*** — *simulatio simplicitatis* — The feigning of simplicity. ***τὸ εἶναι βαυκοπανοῦργον*** — *in cute ovis, intus lupus* — in the skin a sheep, within a wolf [to be a mincing knave].
- ***εὐήθεια*** — *fatuitas* — Silliness.

**III.**

**The virtue:** ***εὐγνωμοσύνη*** — *Candor* — Candour.

**In defect:** ***διαβολή*** — *calumnia* — Slander. ***κάλυμμα ἐπιβουλευτικόν*** — *insidiosa occultatio* — Treacherous concealment.

**In excess:** ***ταχυπείθεια*** — *credulitas nimia et praepropera* — Credulity overmuch and overhasty.

**IIII.**

**The virtue:** ***καρτερία*** — *Constantia* — Constancy.

**In defect:**

- ***ἀστασία*** — *levitas* — Lightness.
- ***ἐποχὴ ἀκαδημική*** — *Suspensio Academica disputatorum scholasticorum* — The Academic suspension of judgement, of the disputers of the schools.

**In excess:** ***αὐθάδεια καὶ ἰσχυρογνωμοσύνη*** — *pertinacia* — Stubbornness.

<!-- p.225 -->

**V.**

**The virtue:** ***εὐμάθεια*** — *Docilitas* — Teachableness. ***εὐσυνεισία*** (quickness of understanding).

**In defect:** ***ἐποχή, διαβολή. αὐθάδεια.*** (suspension of judgement, slander; self-will).

**In excess:** ***κουφότης*** — *levitas* — Lightness.

**VI.**

**The virtue:** ***σιγή, σιωπή, ἐχεμυθία ἡ καλή*** — *Taciturnitas* — Silence [stillness, holding one's peace, the keeping of counsel that is seemly].

**In defect:**

- ***ἀδολεσχία καὶ πολυλογία*** — *garrulitas* — Talkativeness.
- ***φλυαρία τῶν χυδαίων, εἰκαιοσύνη*** — *futilitas, nugacitas, vanitas* — Babbling, trifling, vanity [the prating of the common sort; idle rashness].

**In excess:** ***ἡ οὐ καθήκουσα καὶ οὐκ ἐν δέοντι γενομένη ἐχεμυθία*** — *tacere ubi tacendum non est: et esse mutum canem, cum vocatio flagitat liberam confessionem* — To keep silence where silence is not to be kept, and to be a dumb dog when one's calling demandeth a free confession. ***νηπιέη*** — *infantia* — Speechlessness.

**VII.**

**The virtue:** ***Λογιότης*** — *Eloquentia* — Eloquence.

**In defect:** *Infantia* — Want of utterance.

**In excess:** ***ἀδολεσχία*** — *garrulitas* — Talkativeness. ***πᾶσα κατάχρησις τῆς λογιότητος*** — *omnis abusus eloquentiae* — Every abuse of eloquence.

**VIII.**

**The virtue:** ***Εὐτραπελία, φιλοφροσύνη, μετὰ σεμνότητος χαριέδης[?]*** — *comitas* — Courtesy [ready pleasantness, friendliness, graceful with gravity].

**In defect:** ***δυσχέρεια*** — *morositas* — Moroseness.

**In excess:**

- ***εὐτραπελία***, **Eph. 5**, which is ***μωρολογία, κουφότης, ἀπαιδευσία. καὶ βωμολοχία*** — *scurrilitas et levitas* — Jesting, which is foolish talking, lightness, ill breeding, and buffoonery: scurrility and lightness.
- ***κολακεία*** — *adulatio* — Flattery.

**IX.**

**The virtue:** ***χαριεντισμὸς*** <!-- p.226 -->***καὶ ἀστειότης*** — *Urbanitas, civilitas* — Urbanity, civility.

**In defect:**

- ***ἀπειροκαλία*** — *Insulsitas* — Tastelessness.
- ***ἀναρμοστία καὶ ἀπρέπεια*** — *indecentia* — Unseemliness.
- ***τὸ ἀπροσδιόνυσα λέγειν, ἢ παραφθέγγεσθαι*** — *absurda loqui* — To speak things beside the purpose.

**In excess:** ***βωμολοχία. σπερμολογία καὶ φλυαρία*** — *scurrilitas* — Scurrility [buffoonery; idle prating and babbling].

## *Virtutes Noni et Decimi Praecepti* · The Virtues of the Ninth and Tenth Commandments

***δικαιοσύνη ἡ ἀνέκαθεν, καὶ πρώτη: εἰκὼν τοῦ θεοῦ ἐν ἀνθρώποις*** — *Iustitia originalis. Imago Dei in hominibus* — Original righteousness. The image of God in men.

Contrary to this is all ***ἁμαρτία, ἐπιθυμία, ἀπειροπάθεια, ἢ τῶν παθῶν ἀμετρία, καὶ ἀταξία. πᾶσα μοχθηρία, καὶ ἐννόησις τῆς ἀδικίας, ἡ ὄρεξις, ὁρμή, βούλευσις, ἐπιβολή*** — *omnis inobedientia et transgressio* — all disobedience and transgression [sin, concupiscence, boundless passion, or the want of measure and of order in the affections; all wickedness, and the very thinking upon unrighteousness, the appetite, the impulse, the purposing, the setting about it].
