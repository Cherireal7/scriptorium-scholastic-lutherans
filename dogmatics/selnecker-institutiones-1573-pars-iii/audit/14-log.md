## Doubtful, not changed

- p.280 (img 286) — "Cœlos penetrauit" rendered "He hath passed through the Heavens"; KJV Heb. 4:14 has "passed into the heavens". Left, since Selnecker's own use of penetrare on p.281 ("quos omnes penetrauit") requires "through".
- p.280 (img 286) — "Spes viua & immarceßibilis gloriæ & salutis æternæ": "immarcessibilis" may go with "spes" ("a lively and unfading hope of glory") or with "gloriae" (as translated, after 1 Pet. 1:4). Left.
- p.294 (img 300) — "vmbraculum Ecclesiæ" rendered by a doublet, "the shadow and covert of the Church". Defensible; left.
- p.291 (img 297) — the first of the ten final causes has no numeral in the print; the translation numbers it "1." Left.
- Endnote F3-1 (Rufinus, Expositio symboli, as the source of the "Cyprian" hook saying and of the two sayings on the ascension and session): kept; I recognise all three from Rufinus's exposition, which passed under Cyprian's name in the sixteenth-century editions, but I could not check a text of Rufinus here. The third "Cyprian" saying on p.285 ("Inuisibilem Deum, visibilem Filium recepisse…") is not from Rufinus and the note does not claim it.
- Endnotes F3-4 (Ephes. 1 for 1 Cor. 11:3), F3-6 (condemnationem for condonationem) and F3-7 (Esa. 45, twice, for Isa. 43:25): readings confirmed on the images; kept unchanged.
