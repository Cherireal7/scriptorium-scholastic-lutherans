## Doubtful, not changed

- p.255 (img 261) — εἰς τῷ ἠγαπημένῳ is a solecism of the print (εἰς with a dative; Eph. 1:6 reads ἐν τῷ ἠγαπημένῳ). Given as printed, without a new endnote; the editor may wish to add one or restore ἐν with a note.
- p.259 (img 265) — ἀλογίαν (Apollinarists): confirmed on the enlarged image, no change.

- p.253 (img 259) — "exaudit" rendered "he heareth prayer" ("prayer" is supplied); defensible.
- p.263 (img 269) — "**Hugh** [of St Victor]": the bracketed identification is the translator's; the print has only "Hugo". Correct (the passage is Hugh of St Victor's), kept in brackets.
