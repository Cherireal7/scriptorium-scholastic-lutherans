# Audit log — part 01 (A1: title page; Luther's Small Catechism, pp. 1–16)

- title (img 005) — print: "Sanctifica eos in veritate tua: sermo tuus est veritas" — was: "Sanctify them in thy truth: thy word is truth." — now: "Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth." — kind: scripture
- p.1 (img 007) — print: "Non habebis Deos alienos coram me" — was: "Thou shalt not have strange gods before me." — now: "Thou shalt have no other gods before me." — kind: scripture
- p.2 (img 008) — print: "Memento, ut diem Sabbati sanctifices" — was: "Remember that thou sanctify the Sabbath day." — now: "Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy." — kind: scripture
- p.4 (img 010) — print: "Non furtum facies" — was: "Thou shalt not commit theft." — now: "Thou shalt not steal." — kind: scripture
- p.5 (img 011) — print: "Non loqueris contra proximum tuum falsum testimonium" — was: "Thou shalt not speak false witness against thy neighbour." — now: "Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour." — kind: scripture
- p.7 (img 013) — print: "faciens misericordiam in millia iis, qui diligunt me, et custodiunt praecepta mea" — was: "showing mercy unto thousands, to them that love me and keep my commandments" — now: "showing mercy unto thousands of them that love me, and keep my commandments" — kind: scripture
- p.12 (img 018) — print: "πάντας τεθνεῶντας ἐκ τῶν νεκρῶν" — was: "τεθνεῶτας" — now: "τεθνεῶντας" (the print's own form, with ν) — kind: greek
- p.13 (img 019) — print: "Pater noster qui es in coelis" — was: "Our Father who art in heaven" (heading and text) — now: "Our Father which art in heaven" — kind: scripture
- p.15 (img 023) — print: "Fiat voluntas tua sicut in coelo, ita etiam in terra" — was: "Thy will be done, as in heaven, so also on earth." — now: "Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven." — kind: scripture

## Doubtful, not changed

- p.1 (img 007) — "ὀφείλομεν τὸν θεὸν πρὸ πάντων": the abbreviation could be taken for περὶ; compared with πρὸ αἰώνων (p.10) and περὶ τούτων (p.6) it is πρὸ, which also answers "supra omnia". Left as πρὸ.
- Greek accents: the translator has normalised enclitic and grave accents in places (print "τὶ τοῦτο ἐστὶν;" given as "τί τοῦτό ἐστιν;", and the like). Letters agree with the print; accentuation left as transcribed.
- Greek punctuation: p.5 (img 011) the print has a comma after "τῶν δικαίων" ("μηδὲ τῶν δικαίων, προφάσει"); p.12 (img 018) the print writes "καθεκάστην" as one word (given as "καθ᾽ ἑκάστην"). Left.
- p.3 (img 009) Fourth Commandment: the Latin ("ut bene tibi sit, et ut sis longaevus super terram bonam, quam ... dabit tibi") differs in order and wording from KJV Exod. 20:12 / Deut. 5:16; the English follows the Latin. Left.
- p.6 (img 012) Tenth Commandment: "Non desiderabis ... agrum ... nec ullum iumentum" is fuller than KJV Exod. 20:17 and has "desire" (as KJV Deut. 5:21) against the Ninth's "covet"; the English follows the Latin. Left.
- p.7 (img 013) Exod. 20: "Ego sum Dominus Deus tuus, Deus Zelotes" kept as "I am the Lord thy God, a jealous God" (KJV: "I the LORD thy God am a jealous God"), since the Latin construction differs.
- pp.15–16 (img 023) "venit etiam per se", "fit etiam sine nostra petitione" rendered "cometh indeed", "is done indeed": a loose but defensible rendering of etiam. Left.
- Endnotes A1-1 to A1-13 are all notes on differences between the Greek and the Latin, or on the print's oddities; each was confirmed on the image (τοῦ τὸ πλησίον p.3; τῆς τοῦ σατανᾶς and οἰκεῖς p.10; παρανοεῖν p.6; κάρφει p.15; the asterisked alternatives pp.9, 11). None cut or softened.
