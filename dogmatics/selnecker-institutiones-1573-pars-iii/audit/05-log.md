## Readings and notes confirmed on the image (no change)

- p.78 (img 086, lines 12–18) — the rubbed patch loses only the first two to five letters of seven lines ("[Ets]i", "[catio]", "[atti]net", "inte[rio]re", "[co]rde", "[ab e]o", "[tio]nem", "[im]it"); every restoration is certain and the English is right.
- Misprint notes: C2-3 "1. Reg. 35." (img 080), C2-5 "vocatio" (img 093), C2-10 "obiectionem" (img 098) — the print reads as each note says.
- Greek confirmed letter by letter: ἀνδριαντοπλασία (img 079); σέβασμα, λατρεία, προσκύνησις (img 082); κυριακόν (img 092, print with grave in running text); the pseudo-Ignatius passage (img 095); the Theodoret sentence (img 098).
- Source notes kept as they stand, each verified from knowledge of the text: C2-1 and C2-2 (Wisdom 14; the ascription of Wisdom to Philo in Jerome's preface; Josephus, Ant. 18); C2-4 (Tibullus 1.9.3–4, the couplet is verbatim); C2-6 (the saying of Elias, Talmud and Carion's Chronicle); C2-7 (Irenaeus 5.28.3, verbatim in the old Latin version); C2-8 (Magnesians 9, longer recension; the Psalm titles); C2-9 (Irenaeus 3.19.3). These were checked against memory only, not against editions.

## Doubtful, not changed

- p.72 (img 080) — print: "Valentinus"; the English has "Valentinian" (the emperor is certainly meant). Left; no note says the print is corrected here.
- p.91 (img 099) — print: "ἀνθροπινῶς" (misprint); the English gives the correct form "ἀνθρωπίνως" without a note. Left.
- p.86 (img 094) — print: "typus quietis diei ab operibus creationis", rendered as printed ("the rest of the day from the works of creation"); "diei" is probably a misprint for "Dei" ("the rest of God"). Left as printed.
- p.79 (img 087) — print: "improbantium secus iudicantes", rendered literally ("disapproving those who judge otherwise"); one expects "secus facientes". Left.
- p.71 (img 079) — Lactantius: "Omnino fingendarum similitudinum ratio" rendered "The whole device of feigning likenesses" ("Omnino" is rather "altogether"). Defensible; left.
- p.75 (img 083) — Exod. 20 (threat and promise): Selnecker's Latin ("fortis zelotes", "verba mea", no "upon the children") differs from the KJV; the English follows his Latin. Likewise "Erit sabbatum ex sabbato" (Isa. 66) and "Reliquum est populo sabbatum" (Heb. 4) on p.86, where his argument turns on the word "Sabbath", and "Commenda Deo viam tuam" (p.84, "unto God").
- p.82 (img 090) — "Monetarius [Müntzer]": the bracketed identification is the translator's addition; it is right and was left.
- p.70 (img 078) — the Latin of Selnecker's own verses is printed above the English version; left as the translator set it.
