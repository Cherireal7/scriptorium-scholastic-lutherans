## Doubtful, not changed

- p.168 (img 176) — "εὐνή": confirmed. The print has the ευ ligature (the same sort as in ἐρευνᾶτε on the line above) followed by νὴ; reading εὐνὴ, given in the file as εὐνή.
- p.168 (img 176) — "παιδείᾳ": the print shows παιδεία with no visible iota subscript; the dative of the file matches the parallel ἐπανορθώσει and was left.
- p.170 (img 178) — "(Baptism, τὸ ἐπερώτημα, the answer, of a good conscience)": "the answer" is the translator's gloss (KJV word of 1 Pet. 3:21) set inside Selnecker's parenthesis without brackets; left.
- p.172 (img 180) — Psal. 19 "Lex Domini immaculata": rendered "undefiled" after the Latin, where the KJV has "perfect"; left as following the Latin.
- p.170 (img 178) — Psal. 116 "in consternatione mea": rendered "in my consternation" (KJV "in my haste"); left as following the Latin.
- p.173 (img 181) — "Deuteronom. 21" is what the print reads (the passage meant is Deut. 31); kept as printed, no note.
- p.176 (img 184) — "tanquam ministri fidei": "as ministers of faith" (taken with fructus); the Latin is ambiguous, left.
- p.179 (img 187) — "aliquando": Selnecker very probably means "in some measure" (the next clause limits it to conscience and outward actions), but the word printed is aliquando; the English now follows the print.
- p.181 (img 189) — print "obiectio dignior" (for obiecto) and "ex tota menta tua" (for mente): evident misprints, translated correctly, no note.
- endnote D1-3 — that the edition is dated 1579 rests on the title page, which lies outside this part; the "1572" of the text is confirmed on img 180.
