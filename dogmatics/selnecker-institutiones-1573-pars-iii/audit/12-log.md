# Audit log — part 12 (F1, Creed 1), printed pp. 226–251, images p00232–p00257

- p.228 (img 234) — print: "sumat ex eo potum vitae" (ex eo = from the depositorium) — was: "may take from her the drink of life" — now: "may take from it the drink of life" — kind: misreading (the English followed Irenaeus's received text "ex ea", not the print)
- p.236 (img 242) — print: "beneficia" (confirmed), definition of God — was: note F1-6 "The definition is Philip Melanchthon's, from the Loci (De Deo) and the Examen ordinandorum." — now: "in substance Philip Melanchthon's, found with small differences of wording in the later editions of the Loci (De Deo) and in the Examen ordinandorum." — kind: endnote (softened; Selnecker's wording is not identical with either place)
- p.239 (img 245) — print: "Nam πόρος motum significat" — was: "For πόρος signifieth also motion" — now: "For πόρος signifieth motion" — kind: addition
- p.246 (img 252) — print: "Quod sunt gradus rerum creatarum?" (Quod a slip for Quot; the answer begins "Quatuor") — was: "What are the degrees of created things?" — now: "How many are the degrees of created things?" — kind: mistranslation

## Doubtful, not changed
- p.229 (img 235) Apostles' Creed, art. 1 "creatorem coeli et terrae" is given in the traditional form "maker of heaven and earth", while the Nicene "factorem" is also "maker" and p.241 has "Creator of heaven and earth". Traditional wording; left.
- p.240 (img 246) "qui locutus est per Prophetas IN vnam catholicam & Apostolicam Ecclesiam" (accusative) is rendered "IN one Catholic and Apostolic Church". "Unto" would give the case, but the argument turns on the word IN; left.
- p.231 (img 237) "descendit in Iordanem" rendered "came down upon Jordan" (Greek ἐπί); left.
- p.233 (img 239) "coaequales per omnia, vt dictum est: & Vnitas…" — the print shortens the received text; the English "coequal in all things, as is aforesaid: both the Unity…" follows the print's punctuation; left.
- p.238 (img 244) οὐσιῶδες: the accent in the print may be an acute (οὐσιώδες); too small to decide; left.
- p.245 (img 251) Ps. 33 "spiritu oris eius" rendered "by the Spirit of his mouth" (KJV "breath"): required by Selnecker's argument for the three persons; left.
