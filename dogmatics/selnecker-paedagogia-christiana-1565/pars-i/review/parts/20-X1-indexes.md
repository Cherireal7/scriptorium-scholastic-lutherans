# *Elenchus locorum Sacrae Scripturae, qui in prima parte huius Operis obiter explicati sunt* · A List of the Places of Holy Scripture which are Expounded by the Way in the First Part of this Work

- **Gen. 2** — *"God rested on the seventh day from all his work."* — 102
- **Gen. 2** — The Lord said: *"It is not good,"* both for the human kind and for each several person, *"to be alone; but let us make him an help."* — 210
- **Gen. 3** — *"In the sweat of thy face shalt thou eat bread, till thou return unto the ground; for out of it wast thou taken: for dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return."* — 215
- **Gen. 3** — *"I will greatly multiply thy sorrows, when thou shalt be with child; in sorrow thou shalt bring forth children."* — 242
- **Gen. 3** — *"And thy husband shall rule over thee."* — 217
- **Deut. 23** — God forbiddeth that any man lend upon usury to his brother, but permitteth it toward the stranger and foreigner. — 241
- **2 Sam. 15** — Absalom the seditious *"stole the hearts of the men of Israel."* — 200
- **Ps. 133** — *"Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity."* — 149
- **Ps. 128** — *"Thou shalt eat the labour of thine hands: happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee."* — 216
- **Isa. 66** — *"From one sabbath to another."* — 106
- **Matt. 5** — *"Ye have heard that it was said by them of old time, Thou shalt not kill."* — 175
- **Luke 6** — *"Do good, and lend, hoping for nothing again."* — 242
- **Luke 14** — *"Compel,"* or force, men *"to come in,"* and to come to my feast. — 335
- **Luke 18** — *"None is good, save one, that is, God."* — 376
- **John 17** — *"Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth."* — 25
- **Rom. 1 & 2** — Men hold the truth captive in unrighteousness. — 29
- **Rom. 8** — The affections of the flesh are enmity against God. — 304
- **1 Cor. 2** — *"The natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God."* — 309
- **1 Cor. 7** — They that use this world, let them be as though they used it not. — 224
- **Eph. 5** — Let him love his wife as his own body, and as the companion and help of his life. — 216
- **Eph. 6** — *"Ye masters, do the same things unto"* your servants, *"forbearing threatening: knowing that your Master also is in heaven; neither is there respect of persons with"* God. — 164
- **Phil. 2** — *"Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling."* — 343
- **Phil. 3** — The godly shall be partakers of incorruption, and of immortality, and of glorification. — 383
- **Col. 4** — *"Masters, give unto your servants that which is just and equal."* — 164
- **Col. 2** — See that no man deceive you through philosophy. — 375
- **1 John 5** — There are three that bear witness in earth, *"the Spirit, and the water, and the blood,"* and these three are one. — 28
