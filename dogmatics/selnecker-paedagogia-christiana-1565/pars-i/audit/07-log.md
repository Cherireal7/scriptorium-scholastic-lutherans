## Doubtful, not changed
- p.260: κτεάνων. Under enlargement the blurred word looks like κτε + a χ-like letter + an accented letter + ων. The OCR reads "ηζεανων", and κτεάνων fits both sense and metre, so it is kept.
- p.249: I checked the passage on the Jews word for word against the print (*quod sint blasphemi … quod sint manifesti fures et usurarii, quod sint infensissimi sanguini Christianorum, et saepe etiam puellos interficiant, ut sanguine potiantur … proditores … nimis grande sit*). The English renders it exactly, neither softened nor sharpened. "their blood" for *sanguine* is the natural sense. Endnote P12-6 is kept as written; nothing in it is factually wrong.
- p.244/245: endnote P12-1 says the print reads *tunc* where *non* is required. The print does read *tunc*. Reading it as *non* is a conjecture (*tum* is also possible). The note says what the print reads, so it is kept.
- p.247: "runneth secretly through all his members" for *per omnia decurrit*: "his members" is supplied, but this is defensible. Not changed.
- p.269 "1 Reg. 15" rendered "1 Kings [1 Sam.] 15", and p.273 "1 Reg. 18" rendered "1 Kings 18" (Ahab). These are Selnecker's own slips, so the references stay as printed.
- 14-P14.md line 1 also carries "<!-- p.282 -->". It is now a duplicate and should be removed by whoever holds part 14 (not edited here).
