## Doubtful, not changed

- Join 08/09 at p.182: checked against the image. Part 08 ends with Nazianzen's verses and "He easily giveth pardon, who hath need of pardon" (p.182, line 15 of the print). Part 09 begins with the next paragraph, "Haec de ira breuiter dicta sint". Nothing is lost and nothing is doubled. The joins with 07 (p.161, Seneca) and 10 (p.201, "Praetereo iam") are also clean.
- p.174 "νεμεσίς τε": the print has νεμεσι + a στ-ligature + ε, which is σ+τ. The reading stands. The accent on ι is faint.
- p.174 "ὅσσα τὲ": the print shows ὅ followed by two sigma forms, then α τὲ. The reading stands.
- p.179 "καταδαίνυτο": the print shows κατ-ligature + α + δαίνυτο (not κατεδαίνυτο). Kept as printed.
- p.176 "χέτλιος": at enlargement the print shows a plain χ with no σ. A σχ-ligature cannot be ruled out entirely.
