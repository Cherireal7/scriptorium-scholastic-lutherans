## Doubtful, not changed

- p.336 — the print's ***ἀνάγκασον*** has its breathing set over the ν, so that it looks like αὐάγκασον; read as a misplaced breathing and left as ἀνάγκασον.
- p.349 — ***ἁρμοστὰς***: the accent over the final α is a short stroke that could be acute or grave, and the breathing is unclear; left as the translator has it.
- p.357 — ***Ἔχει θεὸς ἔκδικον ὄμμα***: the capital Ε shows no breathing on the image and ἐκδικον may lack its accent; the normal forms are left.
- p.358 — Plato, Cephalus: the print seems to read ἀγαθη without an accent; left as ***ἀγαθὴ***.
- p.346 — "ante agonem dixerat" rendered "before his last agony"; "last" is not in the Latin but gives the sense (Cruciger's deathbed); left.
- p.350 — endnote P17-4 (print *effusionem*, apparently for *confusionem*): the print reads *effudit super illum effusionem*, as the note says; kept.
- The three `##` headings "*De Quarto* … · Of the Fourth Point" (part 16) and "*De Sexto* … · On the Sixth Point" (part 17) use "Of" and "On"; not an error, not changed.
- Part 17's "# *Appendix*" uses a top-level heading; left for the editor.
