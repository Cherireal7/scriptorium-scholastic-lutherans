# Appendix: The 1561 and 1573 Editions Compared
*Francofordiae ad Oderam 1561 — Lipsiae 1573*

The edition printed at Frankfurt on the Oder in 1561 (Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Asc. 5561 x; digital copy bsb10173614) was laid beside the Leipzig edition of 1573 translated in this volume. The result is as follows.

## What the two editions share

Both have the same eighteen sections, under the same headings and in the same order. Every prayer, psalm and hymn printed in 1561 stands also in 1573, in the same place. Nothing printed in 1561 is wanting in 1573. In eighteen prayers compared on the pages of both editions the text is the same, with small differences of a word or two that do not change the sense of a prayer; the whole text has not been compared word for word.

## What differs

The 1561 edition has its own dedication, to Duke Johann Albrecht of Mecklenburg, dated 3 March 1561; it is translated in the preceding appendix. The 1573 edition has in its place the preface dated 1570. The 1561 edition has no calendar and no printed index.

The 1573 edition adds ninety-four pieces, most of them short, about thirty-five of its 262 pages. They stand at the end of ten of the sections:

| Section | Pages of 1573 | Pieces | What is added |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | 14–16 | 3 | Introit, collect, Prose of the Trinity |
| II | 34–36 | 6 | Five collects; the hymn *Benedictus es* |
| IV | 62–64 | 8 | Collects |
| V | 79–84 | 12 | *Corde natus*; the Prose on the Nativity; ten collects |
| VI | 109–122 | 24 | Four hymns; Bonaventure’s prayer; two collects; the fifteen prayers beginning “O Jesu”; the Memorial of the Passion with its collect |
| VII | 129–137 | 10 | Three hymns; two sequences; the *Benedicamus*; three collects; Gregory |
| X | 189–194 | 17 | Collects and the short forms from “For Acceptable Speech” to “For Those on a Journey” |
| XI | 201–204 | 10 | Collects |
| XV | 245–246 | 2 | Two prayers |
| XVIII | 262 | 2 | The two table prayers in verse |

Sections III, VIII, IX, XII, XIII, XIV, XVI and XVII are the same in both editions.
