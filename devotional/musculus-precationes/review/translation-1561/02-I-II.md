# I. Forms of Invoking the Holy Trinity
*Formulae invocandi Sanctam Trinitatem*

## Augustine, Meditations, chapter 12

{f.1r} O highest Trinity, one power and undivided Majesty, our God, God almighty, I, the last of thy servants and a small member of thy Church, confess unto thee; I confess unto thee, and honour thee with the due sacrifice of praise. And because outward gifts which I might offer are wanting to me, behold, those vows[^1] of praise which are in me by the gift of thy mercy I willingly and joyfully offer unto thee, out of faith unfeigned and a pure conscience. I believe thee therefore with my whole heart, O Lord, King of heaven and earth, and with my mouth I confess thee, Father, and Son, and Holy Ghost, three in persons {f.1v} and one in substance, the true God almighty, of one simple and incorporeal, invisible and uncircumscribed nature, in goodness the highest, in wisdom inestimable, in counsels terrible, in judgments just, most secret in thy thoughts, true in thy words, holy in thy works, plenteous in mercies, most patient toward them that offend, most kind toward the penitent, ever the selfsame, eternal and everlasting, immortal and unchangeable. Thee, God the Father unbegotten, thee, the only-begotten Son, thee, the Holy Ghost the Comforter, the holy and undivided Trinity, with our whole heart and mouth we confess, praise and bless; to thee be glory for ever and ever. Amen.

## Augustine, Meditations, chapter 32

I call upon thee, O blessed Trinity, that thou wouldest come unto me, and make me a temple worthy of thy glory. I ask the Father through the {f.2r} Son, I ask the Son through the Father, I ask the Holy Ghost through the Father and the Son, that all vices may be put far from me, and all holy virtues be planted in me. Keep me, the work of thy hands, who hope in thee and trust[^2] in thy mercy alone. Thou art God almighty, the keeper and protector of all that hope in thee, without whom no man is safe, no man delivered out of perils. Thou art God, and there is no other God beside thee, neither in heaven above nor in earth beneath. Thee praise becometh, thee honour becometh, thee a hymn becometh; to thee all Angels, to thee the heavens and all the powers sing hymns and without ceasing chant praises together, as creatures to their Creator, servants to their Lord, soldiers to their King. Thee, the holy and undivided Trinity, every creature doth magnify, every spirit doth praise.

## Augustine, Meditations, chapter 33

Thou art God almighty, whom we worship and adore as {f.2v} three in persons and one in the substance of the Godhead: the Father unbegotten, the Son the only-begotten of the Father, the Holy Ghost proceeding from both and abiding in both, the holy and undivided Trinity, one God almighty; who, when we were not, didst mightily make us, and when we had been lost by our own fault, by thy lovingkindness and goodness didst wondrously recover us. Suffer us not, I beseech thee, to be unthankful for so great benefits and unworthy of so many mercies. I pray thee, I entreat and ask thee, increase our faith, increase our hope, increase our charity. Make us by that same grace of thine to be ever steadfast in faith and effectual in work, that through a right faith we may, by thy mercy, attain unto life eternal; that there, beholding thy glory as it is, we may adore thy Majesty, and may say together, we whom thou shalt have made worthy to see thy glory: Glory be to the Father, who created us; Glory be to the Son, who redeemed us; Glory be to the Holy Ghost, who {f.3r} sanctified us; Glory be to the highest and undivided Trinity, whose works are inseparable, whose dominion abideth without end. Thee praise becometh, thee a hymn becometh. To thee is due all honour, to thee blessing and brightness, to thee thanksgiving, to thee honour, power and might, unto our God for ever and ever. Amen.

## Augustine, Meditations, chapter 40

O Lord God almighty, who art three and one, who art always in all things, and wast[^3] before all things, and shalt be always in all things, God blessed for ever: to thee I commend this day and at all times my soul, my body, my sight, hearing, taste, smell and touch, all my thoughts, affections, words, deeds, all things outward and inward, my sense and understanding, into the hands of thy power, that thou mayest keep them by day and by night, every hour and every moment. Hear me, {f.3v} O holy Trinity, and preserve me from all evil, from all offence, and from all deadly sin, from all the snares and assaults of devils and of enemies, visible and invisible. Drive out from me the boastfulness of the mind, and increase the compunction of the heart; diminish my pride, and perfect in me true humility; stir up in me weeping; soften my hard and stony heart. Deliver me, O Lord, and my soul, from all the snares of the enemy, and preserve me in thy will. Teach me, O Lord, to do thy will, for thou art my God. Hear, my Lord and my God, hear what I ask, and grant that thou mayest hearken unto that which I ask. If thou despise me, I perish; if thou regard me, I live. If thou require my righteousness, I am dead and stink; if thou look upon me with mercy, thou raisest up him that stinketh from the grave. That which thou hatest in me, put far from me, and engraft in me the spirit of chastity and continence, that whatsoever I shall ask, in the very asking I may not offend thee. Take from me that which {f.4r} hurteth, and give that which may help. Grant me, O Lord, that I may be able always to hold fast brotherly charity, and not forget mine own evils, nor search out those of others. Spare my soul, spare my sins, spare mine offences. Visit me, who am weak; heal me, who am sick; cure me, who languish; and raise me up, who am dead. Have mercy upon me, O Lord God, have mercy upon me, and look upon me from the holy seat of thy Majesty, and lighten the darkness of my heart with the beam of thy brightness. I ask of thee, O Lord, the remission of all my sins, from whom and through whom let there be propitiation for me in the time of my need and distress. Amen.

## Augustine, Soliloquies, chapter 37

O three coequal and coeternal persons, one and true God, Father, and Son, and Holy Ghost, who alone inhabitest eternity and light unapproachable; who hast founded the earth by thy power, and rulest the world by thy {f.4v} wisdom. Holy, holy, holy, Lord God of Sabaoth: terrible and strong, just and merciful; one God, three persons, one essence, power, wisdom, goodness; one and undivided Trinity. Open to me, who cry, the gates of righteousness, and going into them I will praise thee, the Lord. Behold, at thy door, O highest Master of the house, I knock, a beggar; command that it be opened unto him that knocketh, thou who hast said: Knock, and it shall be opened unto you. For at thy door, O most merciful Father, do knock the desires of my roaring inward parts and the cries of the tears of mine eyes. All my desire is before thee, and my groaning is not hid from thee. And thou, O Lord, turn not thy face away from me, neither turn aside in anger from thy servant. O Father of mercies, hear the wailing of thine orphan, and stretch forth thy hand, that best of helpers, that it may draw me back out of the depths of the waters, out of the pit of misery, and out of the miry clay, lest I perish while the mercy of thine {f.5r} eyes looketh on, while the tenderness of thy bowels beholdeth; but let me escape unto thee, my Lord, and see the riches of thy kingdom, and behold thy face for ever, and sing praise unto thy holy name, O Lord, who doest wonders, who makest my heart glad with the remembrance of thee, who enlightenest my youth: despise not mine old age, but make my bones to rejoice, and make my years to live again like the eagle's; who livest and reignest, O holy Trinity, for ever. Amen.

## The Church

O holy Trinity, one God, have mercy upon us. O Lord almighty, eternal, ineffable, and without beginning, whom we confess one in Trinity and three in Unity, thee I praise, thee I adore, thee I glorify, and I render thanks unto thy clemency, who, when I was in the night of darkness, didst will that I should be made partaker of thy grace and mercy. Perfect in me, I beseech thee, the work of thy mercy which thou hast begun, and keep me {f.5v} everywhere by thy free lovingkindness, and make me, a sinner, always to think, speak and do the things that are pleasing unto thee, and after the end of this life make me to attain unto the sight of thee, who in perfect Trinity livest and reignest for ever and ever. Amen.

## The Church

O eternal God, who with thine only-begotten Son and the Holy Ghost art one God, art one Lord, not in the singleness of one person, but in the Trinity of one substance. For that which, by thy revealing, we believe of thy glory, the same we hold of thy Son, the same of the Holy Ghost, without any difference of distinction; so that in the confession of the true and everlasting Godhead there is adored both distinctness in the persons, and unity in the essence, and equality in the Majesty. Which the Angels praise, the Dominions adore, the Powers tremble at; the heavens, the powers of the heavens, and the blessed Seraphim celebrate together with united exultation. With whom we pray thee that thou wouldest command our voices also to be admitted, {f.6r} saying: Holy, Holy, Holy, Lord God of Sabaoth.

## The Church

Almighty everlasting God, who hast given unto thy servants, in the confession of the true faith, to acknowledge the glory of the eternal Trinity, and in the power of the Majesty to worship the Unity: we beseech thee, that by the steadfastness of the same faith we may evermore be defended from all adversities. Amen.

## The Creed of Athanasius the Bishop
*Symbolum Athanasii Episcopi*

Whosoever will be saved, before all things it is necessary that he hold the Catholic faith.

Which faith except every one do keep whole and undefiled, without doubt he shall perish everlastingly.

And the Catholic faith is this: that we worship one God in Trinity, and Trinity in Unity;

Neither confounding the persons, nor dividing the substance.

{f.6v} For there is one person of the Father, another of the Son, another of the Holy Ghost;

But of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, there is one divinity, equal glory, coeternal Majesty.

Such as the Father is, such is the Son, such is the Holy Ghost.

The Father uncreated, the Son uncreated, the Holy Ghost uncreated.

The Father infinite, the Son infinite, the Holy Ghost infinite.

The Father eternal, the Son eternal, the Holy Ghost eternal.

And yet not three eternals, but one eternal.

As also not three uncreated, nor three infinites, but one uncreated and one infinite.

Likewise the Father is almighty, the Son almighty, the Holy Ghost almighty.

And yet not three Almighties, but one Almighty.

So the Father is God, the Son God, the Holy Ghost God.

{f.7r} And yet not three Gods, but there is one God.

So the Father is Lord, the Son Lord, the Holy Ghost Lord.

And yet not three Lords, but there is one Lord.

For like as we are compelled by Christian truth to confess every person by himself to be God or Lord,

So are we forbidden by the Catholic religion to say three Gods or Lords.

The Father is made of none, neither created nor begotten.

The Son is of the Father alone, not made nor created, but begotten.

The Holy Ghost is of the Father and the Son, not made, not created, nor begotten, but proceeding.

So there is one Father, not three Fathers; one Son, not three Sons; one Holy Ghost, not three Holy Ghosts.

And in this Trinity nothing is before or after, nothing greater or less;

But the whole three persons are coeternal with one another, and coequal.

{f.7v} So that in all things, as hath now been said above, both the Trinity in Unity and the Unity in Trinity is to be worshipped.

He that will be saved, let him thus think of the Trinity.

But it is necessary to everlasting salvation that he also believe faithfully the incarnation of our Lord Jesus Christ.

The right faith therefore is, that we believe and confess that our Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, is God and man.

He is God, of the substance of the Father, begotten before the worlds; and man, of the substance of his mother, born in the world.

Perfect God, perfect man, subsisting of a reasonable soul and human flesh.

Equal to the Father according to his divinity, less than the Father according to his humanity.

Who, although he be God and man, yet is not two, but one Christ.

One, however, not by confusion[^4] of the {f.8r} divinity into flesh, but by the taking of the humanity into God.

One altogether, not by confusion of substance, but by unity of person.

For as the reasonable soul and flesh is one man, so God and man is one Christ.

Who suffered for our salvation, descended into hell, rose again the third day from the dead.

He ascended into heaven, he sitteth at the right hand of God the Father almighty.

From thence he shall come to judge the quick and the dead.

At whose coming all men shall rise again with their bodies,

And shall give account for their own works.

And they that have done good shall go into life everlasting, but they that have done evil into everlasting fire.

This is the Catholic faith, which except every one believe faithfully and firmly, he cannot be saved.

## The Confession of Augustine and Ambrose
*Te Deum laudamus*

{f.8v} We praise thee, O God; we acknowledge thee to be the Lord.

All the earth doth worship thee, the Father everlasting.

To thee all Angels, to thee the heavens and all the powers,

To thee Cherubim and Seraphim with unceasing voice do cry:

Holy, Holy, Holy, Lord God of Sabaoth.

Heaven and earth are full of the Majesty of thy glory.

Thee the glorious choir of the Apostles,

Thee the praiseworthy number of the Prophets,

Thee the white-robed army of Martyrs doth praise.

Thee the holy Church throughout all the world doth confess:

The Father of infinite Majesty;

Thy true and only Son, worthy of worship;

{f.9r} Also the Holy Ghost, the Comforter.

Thou art the King of glory, O Christ.

Thou art the everlasting Son of the Father.

When thou wouldest take upon thee man, to deliver him, thou didst not abhor the Virgin's womb.

When thou hadst overcome the sting of death, thou didst open the kingdom of heaven to believers.

Thou sittest at the right hand of God, in the glory of the Father.

We believe that thou shalt come to be our Judge.

We therefore pray thee, help thy servants, whom thou hast redeemed with thy precious blood.

Make them to be rewarded with thy saints in glory everlasting.

O Lord, save thy people, and bless thine heritage.

And govern them, and lift them up for ever.

Day by day we bless thee.

And we praise thy name for ever and ever.

Vouchsafe, O Lord, to keep us this day without sin.

{f.9v} Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us.

Let thy mercy, O Lord, be upon us, even as we have hoped in thee.

In thee, O Lord, have I hoped; let me never be confounded. Amen.

# II. To the Father
*Ad Patrem*

## Augustine, Meditations, chapter 5

I call upon thee, O my God, I call upon thee, who art nigh unto all {f.10r} them that call upon thee in truth. For thou art the Truth: teach me, I beseech thee, by thy clemency, O holy Truth, to call upon thee in thee; for how this ought to be done I know not, but I humbly implore to be taught of thee, O blessed Truth. For to be wise apart from thee is to be foolish, but to know thee is to know perfectly. Instruct me, O divine Wisdom, and teach me thy law. For I believe that he whom thou shalt instruct shall be blessed, and whom thou shalt teach out of thy law. I desire to call upon thee; which I pray may be done in truth. What is it to call upon the Truth in truth, but to call upon the Father in the Son? Therefore, holy Father, thy word is truth, and the beginning of thy words is truth. For this is the beginning of thy words, that in the beginning was the Word. In that Beginning I adore thee, the highest Beginning. In that Word of truth I call upon thee, O perfect Truth, that in him thou, who art that selfsame Truth, mayest direct and teach me in the truth. For what is sweeter than to call upon the Begetter in the name of his Only-begotten, to incline the Father to pity by the remembrance of his {f.10v} Son, to soften the King by the naming of his dearest offspring? For so are the guilty wont to be snatched from prisons; so are they that are given over to bonds wont to be set free; so are they that receive the sorrowful sentence of death wont not only to be acquitted, but moreover to obtain uncommon favour, if only they have brought home to the angry princes the love of their beloved offspring. So do little servants that have offended escape the punishments of their masters, when the sweetness of the sons cometh between on their behalf. So I entreat thee, almighty Father, by the love of thine almighty Son: bring my soul out of prison, that I may give thanks unto thy name; deliver me from the bonds of sins, I beg thee by thine only Son, coeternal with thee; and me, whom mine own deserts threaten with a deadly sentence, do thou, appeased by the intercession of thy most precious offspring who sitteth at thy right hand, restore unto life. For what other intercessor I should send unto thee I know not, save him who is the propitiation for our sins, who sitteth at thy right hand, making {f.11r} intercession for us. Behold my Advocate with thee, God the Father. Behold the High Priest, who needeth not to be cleansed by the blood of another, who shineth bathed in his own blood. Behold the holy, well-pleasing and perfect sacrifice, offered and accepted for a sweet-smelling savour. Behold the Lamb without spot, who before his shearers was dumb, who, smitten with buffets, besmeared with spittings, laden with reproaches, opened not his mouth. Behold, he who did no sin hath borne our sins, and hath healed our infirmities with his stripes. Through this highest Mediator, High Priest and Saviour, hear me, most merciful Father; with whom unto thee, and likewise unto the Holy Ghost, be honour, praise and glory for ever. Amen.

## Augustine, Meditations, chapter 16

O God, most merciful and most loving and most gracious lover of men, through that same highest Priest and true High Priest and good {f.11v} Shepherd, who offered himself unto thee for a sacrifice, laying down his life for his flock, I ask thee, through him who sitteth at thy right hand and maketh intercession for us, our Redeemer and Advocate, I entreat thy lovingkindness and goodness, that thou wouldest grant me, together with the same thy Son and the Holy Ghost, to bless and glorify thee in all things, with much contrition of heart and a fountain of tears, with much reverence and trembling. For of those whose substance is one, the gift is one. But forasmuch as the body, which is corruptible, weigheth down the soul, rouse, I beseech thee, my sluggishness with thy goads, and make me to persevere strenuously in thy precepts and praises day and night. Grant that my heart may grow hot within me, and that in my meditation the fire may burn. And because thine only Son himself hath said: No man cometh to me, except the Father which hath sent me draw him [John 6:44], and, No man cometh unto the Father, but by me [John 14:6], I beseech and humbly ask thee, draw me always unto him, that he may at the last {f.12r} bring me unto thee, thither where he himself is, sitting at thy right hand; where thou with him, and he with thee, in the communion of the Holy Ghost, eternally and everlastingly livest and reignest, God, world without end. Amen.

## Augustine, Soliloquies, chapter 11

O God, the true and highest life, from whom, through whom, and in whom all things live, whatsoever do truly and blessedly live: I, thy creature, under the shadow of thy wings will hope in thy goodness, whereby thou hast created me. Help thy creature, which thy kindness hath created. Let not that perish in my wickedness which thy goodness hath wrought; let not that perish in my misery which thy highest mercy hath made. For what profit is there in thy creating, if I go down into my corruption? For hast thou, O God, made all the sons of men in vain? Thou hast created me, O Lord; rule that which thou hast created. {f.12v} Despise not the works of thine own hands, O God. Thou madest me of nothing; if thou rule me not, O Lord, I shall return again to nothing. For as I was not, O Lord, and thou madest me of nothing, so if thou rule me not, I shall yet be brought to nothing in myself. Help me, O Lord, my life, lest I perish in my wickedness. If thou hadst not created me, O Lord, I should not be; because thou hast created me, now I am. If thou rule me not, even now I am not. For it was not my merits, not any grace of mine, that constrained thee to create me, but thy most kind goodness and thy mercy. That love of thine, O Lord my God, which constrained thee to create, let the same, I beseech thee, now constrain thee to rule. For what doth it profit that thy love moved thee to create, if I perish in my misery and thy right hand rule me not? Let that mercy, O Lord my God, constrain thee to save that which is created, which moved thee to create that which is not created. Let that love overcome thee to save, which overcame thee to create; for neither is it now less, since that love is thou thyself, who art ever {f.13r} the same. Thy hand, O Lord, is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither thine ear heavy, that it cannot hear. Hear therefore and save me, most merciful Father, through thy Son, our Lord and Saviour. Amen.

## Augustine, Soliloquies, chapter 12

O Lord, Father and God, the life by which all things live, and without which all things are counted dead, leave me not in wicked thought, and give me not haughtiness of mine eyes. Take away from me evil desires. Lighten mine eyes, that they may see thee, and not be lifted up before thee, O everlasting Glory, but think humbly, not on things too wonderful and above them; and let them see the things that are on the right hand, and not the things that are on the left, away from thee; and let thine eyelids go before my steps, for thine eyelids also try the children of men. Allure[^5] my desire with thy sweetness, which thou hast laid up for them that fear thee, that I may {f.13v} desire thee with everlasting desires, lest the inward taste, enticed and deceived by vain things, put bitter for sweet, and sweet for bitter, darkness for light, and light for darkness.

O Light which no other light seeth, Brightness which no other brightness seeth, Light from whom is all light, forsake me not, lest the shadows of mine ignorance increase, and mine offences be multiplied. Amen.

## Augustine, Soliloquies, chapter 15

I therefore[^6] confess unto thee, O Lord my God, my poverty, and let all thy glory be thine. For thine is all the good that is done by me. O Lord, I confess, as thou hast taught me, that I am nothing else than altogether vanity and the shadow of death, and a certain dark deep, and earth without form and void, which without thy blessing doth bud, yet bringeth forth no fruit save confusion, sin and death. If ever I had any good thing, {f.14r} I received it of thee; whatsoever good thing I have is thine, or I have it of thee. If at any time I stood, by thee I stood; if at any time I fell, by myself I fell; and I had lain for ever in the mire, unless thou hadst raised me up; I had been for ever blind, unless thou hadst enlightened me. When I fell, I had never risen again, unless thou hadst stretched out thy hand unto me. And after thou hadst raised me up, I had always fallen, unless thou hadst upheld me; I had perished full often, unless thou hadst governed me. So always, O Lord, so always hath thy grace and thy mercy gone before me, delivering me from all evils, saving me from those that are past, raising me out of those that are present, and defending me against those to come, cutting off also before me the snares of sins, and taking away the occasions and causes; for unless thou hadst done this for me, I had committed all the sins of the world. For I know, O Lord, that there is no sin that ever a man hath done which another man may not do, if the Creator, by whom man was made, be wanting.[^7] But that I did it not, was thy doing; that I abstained, was thy commanding; and that I believed thee, thou didst {f.14v} pour in the grace. For thou, O Lord, didst rule me for thyself, thou didst keep me for thyself and for me; and that I should not commit adultery, nor any other sin, thou gavest grace and light. The tempter was absent: that he was absent was thy doing. Place and time were wanting: and that these were wanting was thy doing. The tempter was present, the place was not wanting, the time was not wanting: but that I consented not, thou didst hold me back. The tempter came dark, as he is: and that I might despise him, thou didst strengthen me. The tempter came armed and strong: and that he might not overcome me, thou didst bridle him and fortify me. The tempter came transformed into an angel of light: and that he might not deceive me, thou didst rebuke him; and that I might know him, thou didst enlighten me. And do thou deliver us, O Lord, who savest them that hope in thee, that he may grieve over us, and thou mayest be praised in us, O Lord our God, for ever. Amen.

## Augustine, Soliloquies, chapter 18

O Lord my God, my Sanctifier, {f.15r} thou hast commanded me by thy law to love thee with all my heart, with all my soul, with all my mind, with all my strength and with all my powers, yea from the inmost marrow of my heart, at all hours and moments in which I enjoy the good things of thy mercy; for I should always perish, but that thou always rulest me; I should always die, but that thou always quickenest me; and every moment thou bindest me unto thee, while every moment thou bestowest on me thy great benefits. As therefore there is no hour nor instant in all my life in which I enjoy not thy benefit, so there ought to be no moment in which I have thee not before mine eyes in my remembrance, and love thee not with all my strength. But neither am I able to do this, unless thou give it me, of whom is every good gift and every perfect gift, coming down from the Father of lights. For it is not of him that willeth, nor of him that runneth, but of thee that shewest mercy, that we should love thee. Thine, O Lord, is this gift, of whom is every good thing. Command that thou be loved; give what thou commandest, and command what thou wilt. I love thee, O my God, and desire ever to love thee more. {f.15v} For thou art in truth sweeter than all honey, more nourishing than all milk, and brighter than all light. Therefore thou art dearer to me than all gold and silver and precious stone. O Fire that ever burnest and art never quenched, kindle me; let me be wholly kindled by thee, that I may wholly love thee. For he loveth thee less, who loveth anything together with thee which he loveth not for thy sake. I will love thee, O Lord, because thou first lovedst me. And whence shall I have words to declare the tokens of thy most great love toward me, by reason of thine innumerable benefits wherewith thou hast brought me up from the beginning? For after the benefit of creation, when in the beginning thou didst create me of nothing after thine own image, honouring and exalting me among those creatures which thou hast made, and ennobling me with the light of thy countenance, wherewith thou hast sealed the lintel of my heart, whereby thou hast set me apart alike from things without sense and from brute things that have sense, and hast made me a little lower than the angels: this also was a small thing in the sight of thy divinity; for with daily {f.16r} and singular and exceeding great gifts of thy benefits thou hast nourished me without ceasing, and as thy little tender son thou hast suckled and strengthened me at the breasts of thy consolation. For that I might wholly serve thee, thou hast delivered all things whatsoever thou hast made into my service. Thou hast put all things under the feet of man, that man alone might be wholly put under thee. Thou knowest our frame, O our Potter, and we are all as clay in thine hand. Thou hast made us, O Maker of the world: rule us. Thou hast created us: despise us not, for we are thy works. Thou indeed art the Lord our God; we are clay and little worms; we are not able to enter into thine eternities unless thou bring us in, who hast created all things of nothing. We beseech thee therefore, by the bowels of thy mercy, that thou wouldest be pleased, O Lord, to save that which thou hast created; for if thou wilt, thou canst save us, and in thy will is the power of our salvation. Save us therefore, most merciful Father, through our only Saviour, thy Son, who liveth and {f.16v} reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Ghost, God, world without end. Amen.

## Augustine, Soliloquies, chapter 26

O Lord, heavenly Father, remember thy mercy of old, wherewith from the beginning thou hast gone before us with the blessings of thy sweetness. For before I, the son of thine handmaid, was born, O Lord, my hope from my mother's breasts, thou didst go before me, preparing for me the paths wherein I should walk and come unto the glory of thy house. Before thou formedst me in the womb thou knewest me, and before I came forth out of the womb thou didst foreordain concerning me whatsoever pleased thee. But I, standing in this dark night and knowing not these things, fear and trembling are come upon me, while I see dangers hanging over me on every side, and myself compassed about in this life with a multitude of miseries without number. And unless in so great evils thy help were at hand for me, I should despair. But I have a great hope in thee, O most gentle Prince, my God; and the consideration of the {f.17r} multitude of thy mercies, which are with thee, lifteth up my mind; and the foregoing tokens of thy mercy, which went before me ere I was born, and have now shone forth in me in a special manner, admonish my hope concerning the better and more perfect gifts of thy kindness yet to come, which thou reservest for thy friends, that it may rejoice in thee, O Lord my God, with that holy and living joy wherewith thou ever makest glad my youth. Thou hast loved me, O my only love, before I loved thee, and hast created me after thine own image, and hast set me over all thy creatures. Moreover thou makest thine angels spirits for my sake, to whom thou hast given charge to keep me in all my ways, lest haply I dash my foot against a stone. Calling these very things to mind before thee, I confess and praise thee, for these thy benefits are great, wherewith thou hast honoured us, giving us thine angels, the spirits, for our service. Let all thine angels therefore praise thee for this, O Lord; let all thy works give thanks unto thee for this, {f.17v} and let thy saints themselves bless thee. O thou our honour, who, honouring and enriching us exceedingly, hast adorned us with many gifts: to thee be praise and glory for everlasting ages. Amen.

## The Church

O Lord, holy Father, almighty God, who didst ineffably beget before all ages a Son coequal, consubstantial and coeternal with thyself, with whom, and with the Holy Ghost, proceeding from thee and from the same Son, thou hast wondrously created heaven, earth, and all things whatsoever exist, visible and invisible: I give thee thanks, I adore thee, I praise thee,[^8] I bless and magnify thee. Be merciful, I beseech thee, unto me, a most wretched sinner, and despise me not, but defend, protect and govern thy creature, which thou hast delivered from eternal death by the precious blood of thine only-begotten Son, that, being preserved, I may praise thee, and bless and glorify thy holy NAME, from this time forth and for evermore. Amen.

## Psalm 34

{f.18r} I will bless the Lord at all times: his praise shall continually be in my mouth.

My soul shall make her boast in the Lord: that the afflicted may hear, and be glad.

O magnify the Lord with me,[^9] and let us exalt his name together.

I sought the Lord, and he heard me, and delivered me out of all my troubles.

They that look unto him, and flow together unto him, their faces shall not be ashamed.

This poor man cried, and the Lord heard him, and saved him out of all his troubles.

The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear him, and shall deliver them.

O taste and see that the Lord is good: blessed is the man that trusteth in him.

O fear the Lord, all ye his saints: for there is no want to {f.18v} them that fear him.

The rich have lacked, and suffered hunger: but they that seek the Lord shall not want any good thing.

Come, ye children, hearken unto me: I will teach you the fear of the Lord.

What man is he that desireth life, and loveth to see good days?

Keep thy tongue from evil, and thy lips from speaking guile.

Depart from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it.

The eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, and his ears are open unto their prayers.

But the face of the Lord is against them that do evil, to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth.

They cried, and the Lord heard, and delivered them out of all their troubles.

The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a troubled heart; and shall save the humble in spirit.

Many are the afflictions of the righteous: and out of them all the Lord shall deliver them.

{f.19r} He keepeth all their bones: not one of them shall be broken.

Evil shall slay the wicked: and they that hate the righteous shall be guilty.

The Lord shall redeem the souls of his servants: and none of them that trust in him shall be guilty.

## Psalm 91

He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High, and abideth under the shadow of the Almighty,

Shall say unto the Lord, My hope and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.

For he shall deliver thee from the snare of the hunters, and from the deadly pestilence.

He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth is a shield and buckler.

Be not afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow that flieth by day;

Nor for the pestilence that walketh in darkness; nor for the plague that wasteth at noonday.

Though a thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten {f.19v} thousand at thy right hand; yet it shall not come nigh[^10] thee.

Surely with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked.

For thou, O Lord, art my hope: thou hast made the most High the habitation of thy refuge.

No evil shall afflict thee, neither shall any scourge come nigh thy dwelling.

For he hath given his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.

They shall bear thee up in their hands, lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.

Thou shalt tread upon the lion and adder: the young lion and the dragon shalt thou trample under feet.

Because he desireth me, I will deliver him: I will protect him, because he hath known my name.

He hath called upon me, and I will answer him: I am with him in trouble; I will deliver him, and glorify him.

With length of days will I satisfy him, and shew him my salvation.

## Psalm 145

{f.20r} I will extol thee, my God, O king; and I will bless thy name for ever and ever.[^11]

Every day will I bless thee; and I will praise thy name for ever and ever.

Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised; and his greatness is unsearchable.

One generation shall praise thy works to another, and they shall declare thy power.

I will speak of the magnificence of the glory of thy praise, and of the words of thy wondrous works.

And they shall speak of the might of thy terrible acts, and shall declare thy greatness.

They shall abundantly utter the memory of thy goodness, and shall rejoice in thy righteousness.

The Lord is gracious, and full of compassion; slow to anger, and of great mercy.

The Lord is good to all: and his tender mercies are over all his works.

Let all thy works praise thee, O Lord; {f.20v} and let thy saints bless thee.

They shall speak of the glory of thy kingdom, and talk of thy power;

To make known to the sons of men thy power, and the glory of the magnificence of thy kingdom.

Thy kingdom is a kingdom of all ages, and thy dominion endureth throughout all generations.

The Lord lifteth up all that fall, and raiseth up all those that be bowed down.

The eyes of all wait upon thee, O Lord; and thou givest them their meat in due season.

Thou openest thine hand, and fillest every living thing with blessing.

The Lord is righteous in all his ways, and holy in all his works.

The Lord is nigh unto all them that call upon him, to all that call upon him in truth.

He will fulfil the desire of them that fear him: he also will hear their prayer, and will save them.

The Lord preserveth all them that love him: but all the wicked will he destroy.

{f.21r} My mouth shall speak the praise of the Lord: and let all flesh bless his holy name for ever and ever.
