# XVI. Forms of Prayer for Eternal Life
*Formulae precandi pro vita aeterna*

## Augustine, Meditations, chapter 21

{f.130r} I am sore weary of this life, and of this toilsome pilgrimage. This life is a wretched life: a frail life, an uncertain life, a laborious life, an unclean life, a life that is the mistress of evils, full of miseries and errors, which is not to be called life but death, wherein we die every moment, through the manifold failings of our changeableness, by divers kinds of deaths. Happy are they, and they are few, who refuse its familiarity, despise its passing joys, and cast off its fellowship, lest with the sinful life they themselves also be compelled to perish. O thou life which God hath prepared for them that love him, thou living life, blessed life, secure life, tranquil life, beautiful life, holy life, life that knoweth not death, that knoweth not sorrow. Would that, pardon of my sins being granted me, and this burden of the flesh being presently laid down, I might enter into thy joys, there to have true rest, and might go within the {f.130v} glorious and beautiful walls of thy city, there to receive the crown of life from the hand of the Lord; that I might be among those most holy choirs, that with the most blessed spirits I might stand before the glory of the Creator, that I might behold the countenance of Christ present, and might ever look upon that highest, and unspeakable, and uncircumscribed light, and so might be touched by no fear of death, but might rejoice without end in the gift of everlasting incorruption. That thou wouldst grant this to me, a wretched sinner, I beseech thee, almighty God, through thine only Son our Saviour, with whom to thee, and together to the Holy Ghost, be honour, power, praise, and glory, world without end. Amen.

## Augustine, Soliloquies, chapter 35

As the hart panteth after the water brooks, so panteth my soul after thee, O God most gracious and most merciful. My soul hath thirsted for thee, O God, the living fountain: {f.131r} when shall I come and appear before thy face? O fountain of life, vein of living waters, when shall I come to the waters of thy sweetness, out of a land pathless and without water, that I may see thy power and thy glory, and may quench my thirst from the waters of thy mercy? I thirst, O Lord; thou fountain of life, satisfy me; I thirst, O Lord, I thirst for thee, the living God. O when shall I come and appear, O Lord, before thy face? O day glorious and fair, that knoweth no evening, that hath no setting, wherein I shall hear the voice of praise, the voice of rejoicing and of thanksgiving, wherein I shall hear: Enter thou into the joy of thy Lord, enter into joy everlasting, into the house of the Lord thy God, where are great things and unsearchable, marvellous things without number; enter into joy without sorrow, which holdeth eternal gladness, where shall be every good thing, and there shall be no evil thing, where shall be whatsoever thou wilt, and there shall be nothing that thou wouldst not. O joy above joy, joy that surpasseth all joy, outside of which there {f.131v} is no joy. When therefore we shall have come to thee, the fountain of wisdom, to thee, the light that faileth not, to thee, the light that cannot be quenched, that we may see thee no more through a glass, darkly, but face to face, then shall our desire be satisfied with good things; there shall we see, love, and praise; in thy light shall we see thy light. Surpassing glory shall it be when we shall see God in himself, shall see him and have him in ourselves, whom to behold shall have no end. To which most blessed vision do thou bring us, O Lord Jesus Christ, our salvation and our only hope. Amen.

# XVII. Forms of Prayer of Those Rising in the Morning and of Those Going to Bed
*Formulae precandi, mane surgentium et cubitum euntium*[^1]

## Augustine, Meditations, chapter 40

{f.132r} O Lord God almighty, who art three and one, who art always in all things, and wast before all things, and shalt be always in all things, God blessed for ever. To thee I commend this day and at all times my soul, my head, all my actions and thoughts, into the hands of thy power, that thou mayest keep them by day and by night, every hour and every moment. Hear me, O holy Trinity, and preserve me from all evil, and from every offence, and from every mortal sin, and from all the snares and assaults of devils, and of enemies visible and invisible. Teach me this day to do thy will; that which thou hatest in me, put this day far from me. Take from me that which hurteth, and give that which may help. Spare this day and always my soul, spare my sins, spare my offences. Give me this day a heart that may fear thee, a mind that may love thee, an understanding that may understand thee, ears that may hear thee, eyes that may see thee. Give me this day, O Lord, discretion to discern between good {f.132v} and evil, and keep me from all evil, who art[^2] blessed and to be praised for ever. Amen.

## The Church

God be merciful to me a sinner, and keep me; be thou all days and nights my God, God of Abraham, God of Isaac, God of Jacob, and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. Have mercy upon me, and send to my help thy holy Angel, to keep and defend me from all mine enemies. Behold the cross of the Lord; flee,[^3] ye adverse powers; the Lion of the tribe of Judah hath conquered. O Saviour of the world, save us, who by thy cross and blood hast redeemed us; help us, we beseech thee. Holy God, Holy and mighty, Holy and immortal, have mercy upon us, and keep us this day from all evil. Amen.

## The Church

O sweetest Lord Jesus Christ, almighty God, into the hands of thine {f.133r} unspeakable mercy I commend my soul and my body, my senses and my speech, my counsels, my words and all my steps, my thoughts, my works, and all my deeds, all things needful for my body and my soul, my coming in and my going out, my conversation, the course and the end of my life, my death, my rest and my resurrection with thy saints and elect for evermore. Open this day my heart and my lips to bless and glorify thy name, which is blessed above every name. Cleanse my heart from all evil and perverse thoughts, that my lips may continually praise thee, my mind meditate upon thee, my life glorify thee, my soul bless thee; that I, who by thy goodness alone was created to the praise and glory of thy name, may ever, and as long as I shall live in this mortal state, abide devoted to thee, that in the sight of thy divine Majesty I may be found meet to serve thee worthily. Who with God the {f.133v} Father, in the unity of the Holy Ghost, livest and reignest, God, world without end. Amen.

## The Church

I give thee thanks, O holy Lord, almighty Father, everlasting God, who hast vouchsafed to keep me this night through thy great mercy. And I beseech thy boundless clemency, that thou wouldst grant me so to pass the day that is coming, with all humility, meekness, chastity, charity, patience, kindness, fear and carefulness, that my service may be well pleasing unto thee, through him who shall come to judge the quick and the dead, and the world by fire. Keep and preserve me from all evil, from every offence, and from every mortal sin, from all the snares and assaults of devils and of enemies visible and invisible, through our Lord Jesus Christ thy Son, blessed for ever. Amen.

## The Church

{f.134r} O Lord Jesus Christ, who art the true Sun of the world, ever rising, never setting, who by thy health-giving countenance dost beget,[^4] preserve, nourish and gladden all things, both which are in heaven and which are on earth: shine graciously, I beseech thee, upon my soul, that, the night of sins and the mists of errors being scattered, and thou shining before me within, I may go without stumbling through all my life, and may walk honestly, as in the day, pure from the works of darkness. Who with the Father and the Holy Ghost livest and reignest for ever. Amen.

## The Church, toward Night
*Ecclesia sub noctem*

O Lord Jesus Christ, to whose inexhaustible kindness we owe all things, who hast bestowed the most clear light of day upon good and evil alike for the discharge of the duties of their work, and hast graciously given the friendly silence of night for refreshing with rest the strength of our poor bodies, and for washing away the {f.134v} cares of our minds and soothing our sorrows; whereas for them that love thee thou thyself dost perform[^5] all these things far more happily within, before whom, unto all the offices of godliness, thou shinest by the grace of faith[^6] far more brightly than the sun shineth upon the world, whom thy promises suffer never to grow weary;[^7] the consolation of thy Spirit washeth away all the troubles of the mind far more effectually than the sleep of the body, neither doth the whole man rest more safely or more sweetly in any other than in thy mercy, O best Redeemer: I pray that, if I have this day through human heedlessness committed anything which hath offended thine eyes, thou wouldst pardon it according to thy wonted goodness, and wouldst grant withal that this night, thou prospering it, may be happy to me; thou keeping it, pure; thou protecting it, safe from the nightly mockeries of devils; that this sleep may render both body and mind more cheerful to serve thee on the morrow. Moreover, forasmuch as this life hath not any hour that is certain, when its evening shall have come, and that sleep of the body shall press upon us from which we shall not awake until {f.135r} the dead shall be raised at the trumpet of thine Angel, I beseech thee that thou wouldst then lighten the eyes of my soul, lest, my faith being quenched, I fall asleep in everlasting death, but that I may rest in thee, unto whom even the dead live. Who livest and reignest with God the Father and the Holy Ghost for ever. Amen.

In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ crucified I arise, who hath redeemed me with his precious blood; may he rule me, bless me, keep me, and stablish[^8] me in every good work, this day and every day, and after this wretched life bring me unto life eternal. Amen.

## The Church

O Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God, crucified for man, I beseech thee by thy holy wounds, that thou wouldst this day heal all the wounds of my soul, and keep me this day, and all the days of my life, from mortal sin, and from all confusion, temptation, and infamy, from offence, and from every peril of soul and body. {f.135v} Keep me, O gracious God, this day and every day from a sudden and unforeseen evil death. Keep me, almighty God, for ever from the pains of Gehenna, and from the intolerable pains of hell. O merciful God, give me this day and every day a right mind and understanding, to think and to do the things that please thee, and to flee and shun the things that displease thee, so that I may never be shut out from thy glory. O God of mercy, God of pity, God of clemency, whose tender mercies are over all thy works, for the sake of thy holy wounds, and for the sake of thy bitter death, give me a good and holy death, give me to die the death of the righteous, and grant that I may never die except I be perfectly pleasing unto thee, and with the receiving of thy most holy body and blood, so that with right faith and firm hope I may be found meet to pass joyfully unto thee. Amen.

## Prayer of One Going to Bed
*Precatio cubitum euntis*

{f.136r} Almighty, everlasting God, I give thee thanks that thou hast vouchsafed of thy grace to keep me this day. I pray thee that whatsoever I have sinned this day in thinking, in speaking, in doing, in seeing, being deceived by the flesh, the world and the devil, thou wouldst vouchsafe graciously to forgive me through thy passion and cross, and to keep me from every peril of soul and body, that I may rise up unharmed to praise the name of thy Majesty, and may be able to serve thee with a good will, a pure heart, and a chaste body, with true gladness and thanksgiving. Kindle in me the fire of thy charity and fear, and grant me a fervent amendment of my whole life, and grant me so to pass the coming night with a clean heart and body, that rising in the morning I may be able to render unto thee the acceptable obedience of my service. Lighten our darkness, and do thou graciously drive from us all the snares of this whole night, through our Lord Jesus Christ thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the {f.136v} unity of the Holy Ghost, God, world without end. Amen.

### Hymn
*Christe qui lux es et dies*[^9]

| O Christ, who art the light and the day,
| thou uncoverest the darkness of the night,
| and art believed to be the light of light,
| proclaiming the blessed light.

| We pray thee, holy Lord:
| defend us in this night.
| Let our rest be in thee;
| grant us a quiet night.

| Let no heavy sleep rush upon us,
| nor the enemy steal us away,
| nor the flesh, consenting unto him,
| set us guilty before thee.

| Let our eyes take sleep,
| let our heart ever watch unto thee;
| let thy right hand protect
| the servants who love thee.

| Look upon us, our defender,
| restrain them that lie in wait;
| govern thy servants,
| whom thou hast purchased with thy blood.

| {f.137r} Remember us, O Lord,
| in this heavy body;
| thou who art the defender of the soul,
| be present with us, O Lord.

| To God the Father be glory,
| and to his only Son,
| with the Spirit, the Comforter,
| world without end.
| Amen.

# XVIII. Forms of Prayer of Those Dining and Supping
*Formulae precandi, prandentium et coenantium*[^10]

## Blessing of the Table. Chrysostom, To the People of Antioch, Homily 58
*Benedictio mensae. Chrysosto. ad populum Anthiochen. Home. 58*

Blessed art thou, O God, who feedest me from my youth, who givest food to all flesh; fill our hearts with joy and gladness, {f.137v} that we, having all sufficiency, may abound to every good work in Christ Jesus our Lord, with whom to thee be glory, honour, and dominion, with the Holy Ghost, for ever. Amen.

## Chrysostom, in the Same Place: Thanksgiving
*Chrysosto. ibidem: Gratiarum actio*[^11]

Glory be to thee, O Lord; glory be to thee, O Holy One; glory be to thee, O King; for thou hast given us meats for our gladness. Fill us with the Holy Ghost, that we may be found well pleasing in thy sight, not ashamed, when he shall render[^12] to every man according to his works. Amen.

## Blessing

The eyes of all wait upon thee, O Lord; and thou givest them their meat in due season. Thou openest thine hand, and fillest every living thing with blessing. O Lord God, heavenly Father, bless us, and these thy gifts, which of thy[^13] {f.138r} bounty we receive, through Christ our Lord. Amen.

## Thanksgiving

O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever. Who giveth food to all flesh, who giveth to the beasts their food, and to the young ravens which call upon him. He delighteth not in the strength of the horse: he taketh not pleasure in the legs of a man. The Lord taketh pleasure in them that fear him, in those that hope in his mercy.

We give thee thanks, O Lord God the Father, through Jesus Christ our Lord, for all thy benefits, who livest and reignest world without end. Amen.

## Blessing of the Table

{f.138v} Whatsoever is set before us,[^14] and whatsoever shall be set before us, may he bid it be happy and holy, who of his kindness feedeth all things. Amen.

## Another
*Alia*

O Christ, gladdener of all, without whom nothing is truly sweet, [we pray][^15] that thou wouldst vouchsafe to be present at this our feast, and by thy presence to gladden our minds, and to mingle thyself with all our dishes and cups, that nothing may be without the savour of thee, and that thou wouldst glide into our souls. Amen.

## Thanksgiving

We give thee thanks, O heavenly Father, who by thine unspeakable power hast made all things, by thine unsearchable wisdom governest all things, by thine inexhaustible {f.139r} goodness feedest and quickenest all things: grant unto thy children that they may one day drink with thee in thy kingdom that nectar of immortality which thou hast promised and prepared for them that truly love thee, through Jesus Christ. Amen.
