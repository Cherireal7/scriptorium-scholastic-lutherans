# IX. Forms of Prayer for Those Coming to the Holy Supper (Synaxis)
*Formulae precandi accedentium ad Sacram Synaxin*

## Augustine, Soliloquies, chapter 23

{f.83v} O Lord God, Creator of heaven and earth, the only expectation of Israel, the desire after which our heart sigheth daily: make haste, tarry not; arise, hasten and come, that thou mayest bring us out of this prison to give thanks unto thy name, that we may glory in thy light. Open thine ears to the tearful cries of thine orphans, who cry unto thee: Our Father, give us this day our daily bread, in the strength whereof we may walk unto thy holy mount Horeb. And I, a little one among the little ones {f.84r} of thy household, O God, my Father and my strength, when shall I come and appear before thy face, that I who now give thanks unto thee for a time may then give thanks unto thee for ever? I know, O Lord, I know, and I confess that I am unworthy to enter under thy roof; but do it for thine honour’s sake, and put not to confusion thy servant that hopeth in thee. And who shall enter into thy sanctuary to consider thy mighty works, except thou open? And who shall open, if thou shut? Moreover, O everlasting goodness of thy mercy, whereby thou hast made all things whatsoever thou wouldest: thou hast made us, O Framer of the world, rule us; thou hast created us, despise us not, for we are thy works. O thou who art rich in all the good feasts of heavenly fulness, most bountiful giver, O God, give food to the weary, gather the scattered, set free the captive, that with free steps he may come in unto thee, and rest in thee, and be refreshed from thee, the heavenly bread. For thou art the bread and the fountain of life, thou the light of eternal brightness. Come, I pray, into my heart, and make it drunk with the plenty of {f.84v} thy pleasure, that I may forget these temporal things. Help me, O Lord my God, and give gladness in my heart; come unto me, that I may see thee. But the house of my soul is too narrow for me until thou come unto it, that it may be enlarged by thee. It is full of chinks: repair it. It hath very many things that may offend thine eyes; I confess it and know it; but who shall cleanse it, or to what other beside thee shall I cry? From my secret faults deliver me, O Lord, and from those of others spare thy servant. O sweetness of love, let my belly eat thee, and let my inward parts be filled with the nectar of thy love, and let my mind utter a good word. O Charity, my God, sweet honey, snow-white milk, thou art the food of the full-grown: make me to grow in thee, that with a sound palate I may be able to eat thee. Thou art my life whereby I live, the hope to which I cleave, the glory which I long to obtain. Do thou hold my heart, rule my mind, direct my understanding, lift up my love, hold my soul hanging upon thee, and draw the mouth of the spirit that thirsteth for thee unto the streams above. Hungering I have begun to seek thee: let me not be forsaken unfed by thee. Famished I have drawn near: let me not {f.85r} go away unfed from thee. Poor, I have come to the rich; wretched, to the merciful: let me not go away empty and despised, for thy holy name’s sake, which is blessed for ever. Amen.

## Jerome, in his agony *Hiero. in agone*

O Lord Jesus Christ, who am I, that I should be worthy that thou shouldest enter under my roof? Hath a sinful man deserved this? Surely, O Lord, I am not worthy. Am I better than all my fathers? Thou wouldest not shew thyself to Moses for one twinkling of an eye: why dost thou now humble thyself so far as to suffer thyself to come down to a man that is a publican and a sinner? And not only art thou willing to eat with him, but thou biddest that thou thyself be eaten by him. Hail, bread of life, who camest down from heaven, giving life to them that worthily receive thee. Surely he that worthily receiveth thee, although by bodily death his soul be separated from his body, shall never die; for that separation is not death, but a passing from death unto life. Wherefore {f.85v} he that worthily eateth thee, dying in the world, beginneth to live with thee eternally. Thou, the bread of Angels, by the sight of thee dost refresh and glorify the Angels. Thou art the food of the soul, not of the body, making fat the mind, not the belly. Thou changest him that eateth thee[^1] into thyself, that by partaking of thee he may be made God; yet art thou not changed into him, as other bodily food is. But woe to them that receive thee unworthily. O most sacred food, whereof he that truly eateth is made God, is delivered from evils and filled with good things, and without doubt is made immortal. O holy provision for the way of our pilgrimage, whereby from this evil world men come to the fellowship of heaven. Come therefore, faithful soul, rejoice and feast, and tarry not. Be not slothful to feed upon these delights; enjoy this banquet, in which the body of thy Saviour is set forth to be received. Man fell into misery through the food of the forbidden tree; through thee he is raised up[^2] to eternal glory. Amen.

## Ambrose, Prayer 1 before Mass *Ambro. Precatio I. ad Missam*

{f.86r} O supreme Priest and true High Priest, Jesus Christ, who didst offer thyself to God the Father upon the altar of the cross, a pure and spotless sacrifice for us wretched sinners, and who hast given us thy flesh to eat and thy blood to drink, and didst establish this mystery in the power of the Holy Ghost, saying, As often as ye shall do these things, ye shall do them in remembrance of me: I beseech thee by thy precious blood, the great price of our salvation; I beseech thee by this wonderful and unspeakable charity wherewith thou hast vouchsafed so to love us wretched and unworthy ones, that thou didst wash us from our sins in thy blood. Teach me thine unworthy servant, whom among thine other gifts thou hast vouchsafed to call unto thy kingdom, for no merits of mine, but only by the condescension of thy mercy. Teach me, I beseech thee, by thy Spirit, to handle so great a mystery with that reverence and honour, and with that {f.86v} devotion and fear, which are due and fitting. Make me, O Lord Jesus Christ, through thy grace always so to believe and understand, to feel and firmly hold, to speak and think concerning so great a mystery, as is pleasing unto thee and profitable to my soul. For with how great contrition of heart and fountain of tears, O Lord Jesus Christ, with how great reverence and trembling, with how great chastity of body and purity of mind is that divine and heavenly sacrifice to be celebrated, where thy flesh is in truth received, where thy blood is in truth drunk, where the highest things are joined to the lowest, where the presence of the holy Angels is at hand, where thou art Priest and sacrifice, wonderfully and unspeakably appointed. Who shall be able worthily to celebrate this, unless thou, almighty God, make him that offereth worthy? I know, and truly I know, and I confess this very thing to thy lovingkindness, that I am not worthy to draw near to so great a mystery, by reason of my exceeding great sins and my numberless negligences. But I know of a truth, and believe with my whole {f.87r} heart, and confess with my mouth, that thou canst make me worthy, who alone canst make clean him that is conceived of unclean seed, and of sinners make men righteous and holy. By this thine almighty power I beseech thee to grant unto me a sinner to draw near to this heavenly sacrifice with fear and trembling, with purity of heart and a fountain of tears, with spiritual gladness and heavenly joy. Let my mind feel the sweetness of thy most blessed presence, and the watch of thy holy Angels round about me. I beseech thee, O Lord, by that same most holy mystery of thy body and blood, wherewith we are daily in the Church fed and given to drink, washed and sanctified, and made partakers of thy one and supreme Godhead, that thou wouldest give me thy holy virtues, that being filled therewith I may draw near to thine Altar with a good conscience, so that these heavenly Sacraments may become to me salvation and life. For thou hast said with thy holy and blessed mouth: The bread that I will give is my flesh, {f.87v} for the life of the world. And, He that eateth me shall live by me; he dwelleth in me, and I in him. I am the bread of life, which came down from heaven. If any man eat of this bread, he shall live for ever. O sweetest bread, heal the palate of my heart, that I may taste the sweetness of thy love. Heal it of all sickness, that I may love no beauty beside thee. O whitest bread, having in thee all delight and the sweetness of every savour, who dost ever refresh us, let my heart eat thee, and let the inward parts of my soul be filled with the sweetness of thy savour. O holy bread, O living bread, O fair bread, O clean bread, who camest down from heaven and givest life unto the world, come into my heart, and cleanse me from all filthiness of flesh and spirit; enter into my soul, heal and sanctify me within and without. Be thou the defence and the continual health of my body and soul. Drive back from me them that lie in wait for me; let the enemies depart far off from thy presence; that being fenced by thee without and within, I may come by a straight path {f.88r} unto thy kingdom, where, not in mysteries, as is done in this present time, but face to face we shall see thee, when thou shalt have delivered up the kingdom to God the Father, and God shall be all in all. Then shalt thou so satisfy me with thyself, that I shall neither hunger nor thirst for ever. Who with the same God the Father and the Holy Ghost livest and reignest, world without end. Amen.

## The Church

I give thee thanks, O Jesus Christ, for thine unspeakable charity, that thou hast vouchsafed to redeem mankind by thy most holy death; and I pray thee, suffer not thy most sacred blood to have been shed for me in vain, but with thy body feed my soul, with thy blood quicken my spirit, that I may be made a fit member of thy mystical[^3] body, which is the Church, and may never fall away from that most holy covenant which at the last supper, when the bread was distributed and the cup was given, thou didst make with {f.88v} thy chosen disciples, and through them with all who by Baptism have been engrafted into thy fellowship. Amen.

### Another

What tongue or what mind is able to give thee thanks, O Lord Jesus, for thine unspeakable charity toward us? Who, that thou mightest redeem lost man, didst vouchsafe to be made man, and to take upon thyself all the wrongs of our condition; and at last upon the altar of the cross, a Lamb without any spot, didst endure to be made a sacrifice for us, paying the penalties due to our sins, that thou mightest reconcile us to the Father. Yea, both living and dying, thou didst spend, give and devote thyself wholly to us. Nor was thy lovingkindness content with these things; but lest at any time forgetfulness of so great charity should creep upon us, or lest our trust in thee should grow faint, even now, reigning in the heavens, thou dost oftentimes refresh our souls with the food of thy body, and gladden them with the sacred cup of thy blood. Let the Spirit, I beseech thee, purify our heart, {f.89r} that I come not unworthy to the heavenly feast, and to the table which even the Angels themselves must tremble at; but that, thou being poured into the inward parts of my mind, I may grow up in thee, and become more lively by spiritual increase, whereby I may continue in the blessed fellowship of thy mystical body, which thou hast willed to be so one[^4] with thee, even as thou art one with the Father, the Holy Ghost knitting all together; to whom be praise and thanksgiving for evermore. Amen.

## Thanksgiving after Communion *Gratiarum actio post Communionem*

### Psalm 23

The Lord ruleth me, and I shall want nothing.

He hath set me in a place of pasture; he hath led me beside the water of refreshment.

He hath restored my soul; he hath led me in the paths of righteousness for his name’s sake.

{f.89v} Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff, they have comforted me.

Thou hast prepared a table before me, against them that trouble me.

Thou hast anointed my head with oil, and my cup overfloweth unto drunkenness.

And goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life.

And I will dwell in the house of the Lord for length of days.

### Psalm 103

Bless the Lord, O my soul: and all that is within me, bless his holy name.

Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits:

Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases;

Who redeemeth thy life from destruction; who crowneth thee with lovingkindness and tender mercies;

{f.90r} Who satisfieth thy mouth with good things; he reneweth thy youth like the eagle’s.

The Lord executeth righteousness and judgment for all that suffer wrong.

He made known his ways unto Moses, his will unto the children of Israel.

The Lord is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and plenteous in mercy.

He will not always chide: neither will he be angry for ever.

He hath not dealt with us after our sins; nor rewarded us according to our iniquities.

For as the heaven is high above the earth, so hath he made his mercy to prevail over them that fear him.

As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our iniquities from us.

Like as a father pitieth his children, so the Lord hath pitied them that fear him.

For he knoweth our frame; he hath remembered that we are dust.

As for man, his days are as grass: as a flower of the field, so shall he flourish.

{f.90v} For the wind passeth over it, and it shall not abide; and it shall know its place no more.

But the mercy of the Lord is from everlasting to everlasting upon them that fear him, and his righteousness unto children’s children;

To such as keep his covenant, and to those that remember his commandments to do them.

The Lord shall prepare his throne in heaven; and his kingdom shall rule over all.

Bless the Lord, all ye his angels, mighty in strength, that do his word, hearkening unto the voice of his words.

Bless ye the Lord, all ye his hosts; ye ministers of his, that do his pleasure.

Bless the Lord, all his works, in all places of his dominion: bless the Lord, O my soul.

### Psalm 111

{f.91r} I will praise thee, O Lord, with my whole heart, in the council and congregation of the upright.

The works of the Lord are great, to be sought out for all that is to be desired in them.

His work is honourable and glorious: and his righteousness endureth for ever.

He hath made his wonderful works to be remembered: the Lord is gracious and full of compassion.

He hath given meat unto them that fear him: he will ever be mindful of his covenant.

He will shew his people the power of his works, that he may give them the heritage of the heathen.

The works of his hands are verity and judgment; all his commandments are sure.

They stand fast for ever and ever; they are done, and are true and upright.

He sent redemption unto his people: he hath promised that his covenant shall be for ever: holy and reverend is his name.

The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do them: his praise endureth for ever.

### Psalm 116

{f.91v} I love, because the Lord hath heard the voice of my supplication.

Because he hath inclined his ear unto me, therefore in my days will I call upon him.

The cords of death compassed me, and the pains of hell gat hold upon me: I found trouble and sorrow.

Then called I upon the name of the Lord; O Lord, deliver my soul.

Gracious is the Lord, and righteous; yea, our God is merciful.

The Lord preserveth the simple: I was brought low, and he delivered me.

Return unto thy rest, O my soul; for the Lord hath dealt bountifully with thee.

For he hath delivered my soul from death, mine eyes from tears, and my feet from falling.

I will walk before the Lord in the land of the living.

# X. Forms of Prayer for Obedience and Newness of Life
*Formulae precandi pro obedientia et novitate vitae*

## Augustine, Meditations, chapter 1

{f.94v} O Lord my God, grant unto my heart to desire thee, in desiring to seek thee, in seeking to find thee, in finding to love thee. Quench in me the desires of the flesh, and kindle the fire of thy love. Drive out of me the spirit of pride, and graciously grant the treasure of thy humility. Remove from me the fury of anger, and of thy kindness bestow on me the shield of patience. Give me a firm faith, a fitting hope, a constant charity. Turn away from me vanity of mind, inconstancy, the reproaching of neighbours, the wickedness of slanders, the lust of riches, the craving for vainglory, the evil of hypocrisy, the poison of flattery, contempt of the needy, oppression of the weak, the burning of covetousness, the rust of envy, the death of blasphemy. O God, my mercy, I pray thee through thy beloved Son, give me the works of mercy, the endeavours of godliness: to suffer with the afflicted, to counsel the erring, to succour the wretched, to help the needy, to comfort the sorrowful, to relieve the oppressed, {f.95r} to refresh the poor, to forgive my debtors, to spare them that sin against me, to love them that hate me, to render good for evil, to despise no man but to honour all, to imitate the good, to beware of the evil, to embrace virtues, to cast away vices; in adversity patience, in prosperity temperance, a watch for my mouth and a door round about my lips; to tread earthly things under foot, to thirst for heavenly things. Grant, O Lord, the strength of my salvation, that I be not of the number of those who for a while believe, and in time of temptation fall away. Overshadow my head in the day of battle, O my hope in the day of affliction, and my salvation in the time of tribulation. Behold, O Lord, my light and my salvation, I have asked for those things which I need, I have made known what I fear. Grant what I ask, through thy Son our Saviour, who is blessed for ever. Amen.

## Cyprian

*Hagios, Hagios, Hagios*,[^6] O Holy of Holies, FATHER of our fathers, God of Abraham, God of Isaac, God of {f.95v} Jacob, God of the Apostles, God of the Prophets, God of the Virgins, God of them that believe, God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ thy Son: we humbly call upon thee, and likewise we entreat thee, who wast begotten from the mouth of the Most High before the ordering of the world, and wast brought forth by a mystery from the womb of Mary the holy virgin. Vouchsafe to grant unto us who pray the desire of the soul and soundness of heart, that the breast which hath been renewed by the saving laver may from carnal offences abide unharmed. We ask, with faith unimpaired, with mind uncorrupted, with sturdy devotion, with continual working or love, that thou wouldest suffer us to flourish in thy holy Church. For unto thee we bend our knees and bow our necks, in whom Angels and Archangels, thousands of thousands of Martyrs, the choir of the Apostles and the glory of the Prophets rejoice; to whom all birds sing praises, and the tongues of things in heaven, of things on earth and of things under the earth make confession. Unto thee all the waters in heaven give praise; things without sense perceive thee; thou art alone, and beside thee there is {f.96r} none. We beseech thee, O Lord, Father almighty, who art to be seen by the Son alone, whom Angels and Archangels obey; we pray thee to bestow on us a sound mind, innocence, a devout sincerity, a conscience holy, pure, sober and chaste, living in glorious faith against all the snares of the world; grant us a breast fenced against the threats of the Devil and the allurements of the flesh, that we be not entangled in violent, bloody and deadly snares, that we may bear the sign of everlasting salvation unharmed to the end. Drive from us all the riot and filth of the world, and every persuasion of the Devil. Thou who sittest above the Cherubim and above the thrones at the right hand of the Father, look upon us, and deliver us from the destruction of eternal death, One in One, the Father in the Son, the Son in the Father, the Holy Ghost with the Father and the Son; through whom and with whom there is unto thee in the holy Church honour, power, glory, majesty, dominion, immortality, both now and for ever, always and unto all ages of ages. Amen.

## The Church

{f.96v} O God, the protector of them that hope in thee, without whom nothing is strong, nothing is holy: multiply upon us thy mercy, that, thou being our ruler and thou our guide, we may so pass through temporal good things, that we lose not the eternal. Through our Lord Jesus Christ thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Ghost, God, world without end. Amen.

## A Prayer for Newness of Life, Gathered out of the Psalms *Precatio pro novitate vitae ex Psalmis collecta*

Unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul. O my God, I trust in thee: let me not be ashamed.

Shew me thy ways, and teach me thy paths. Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art my Saviour; in thee have I hoped all the day.

{f.97r} Teach me thy way, O Lord, and lead me in a plain path, because of mine enemies.

Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.

Cast me not away from thy presence; and take not thy spirit from me.

Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation; and uphold me with thy free spirit.

Teach me thy way, O Lord; and I will walk in thy truth.

Thou bindest my heart, that it may fear thy name.[^7] Give strength unto thy servant, and save the son of thine handmaid.

Deal bountifully with thy servant, that I may live, and keep thy words.

Make me to understand the ways of thy precepts: so shall I talk of thy wondrous works.

Remove from me the way of lying: and grant me thy law.

Set before me for a law, O Lord, the way of thy statutes, that I may keep them always.

Give me understanding, that I may keep thy law, and observe it with my whole heart.

{f.97v} Make me to go in the path of thy commandments; for therein do I delight.

Incline my heart unto thy testimonies, and not to covetousness.

Let not the foot of pride come against me, and let not the hand of the sinner remove me.

Turn away mine eyes from beholding vanity; quicken thou me in thy way. Stablish thy word unto thy servant, that I may fear thee.

Behold, I have longed after thy precepts: quicken me in thy righteousness.

And let thy mercy come upon me, O Lord, even thy salvation, according to thy word.

Teach me goodness, and discipline, and knowledge: for I have believed thy commandments.

Let thy merciful kindness be for my comfort, according to thy word unto thy servant.

Let thy tender mercies come unto me, that I may live. Let my heart be undefiled in thy statutes, that I be not ashamed.

I am thine, save me. Uphold me according unto thy word, that I may live: and let me not be ashamed of my hope.

{f.98r} Help me, and I shall be safe: and I will delight in thy statutes continually.

Order my steps according to thy word: and let not any iniquity have dominion over me.

Hold up my goings in thy paths, that my footsteps slip not.

Deliver me from the false accusations of men: so will I keep thy precepts.

Make thy face to shine upon thy servant; and teach me thy statutes.

Set a watch, O Lord, before my mouth; keep the door of my lips.

Incline not my heart to any evil thing, that I be not busied with works of iniquity.

Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God: let thy good spirit lead me into the right way.

And take not the word of truth ever out of my mouth; for I have hoped in thy judgments.

Lord, make me to know mine end, and the number of my days, what it is; that I may know that I shall cease to be.

# XI. Forms of Prayer under the Cross and Affliction
*Formulae precandi sub cruce et afflictione*

## Augustine, Meditations, chapter 38

{f.98v} Have mercy, O Lord, have mercy, graciously have mercy upon me a most wretched sinner, who do unworthy things and suffer worthy things, who sin continually and daily bear thy scourges. If I weigh the evil that I have done, that which I suffer is not so great; that which I have committed is heavier, that which I bear is lighter. Thou art just, O Lord, and thy judgment is right; all thy judgments are just and true. Just and upright art thou, O Lord our God, and there is no iniquity in thee. For not unjustly nor cruelly dost thou afflict us sinners, O almighty and merciful Lord. I know and am sure that our life is not driven by chance motions,[^8] but is ordered and governed by thee, {f.99r} the Lord our God. Wherefore thou carest for all, and chiefly for thy servants, who have put their whole hope in thy mercy alone. Therefore I beseech and humbly pray thee, that thou deal not with me according to my sins, whereby I have deserved thy wrath, but according to thy great mercy, which surpasseth even the sins of the whole world. Thou, O Lord, who layest scourges upon me without, give always within a patience that faileth not, so that thy praise depart not from my mouth. Have mercy upon me, O Lord, have mercy upon me, and help me, as thou knowest what is needful for me in body and soul. Thou knowest all things, thou canst do all things, who livest for ever. Amen.

## Bernard

Hail, most gracious Lord Jesus Christ, conqueror of death, giver of graces, the hope and comforter of all the sorrowful: have mercy upon me, and comfort me in all my tribulations and necessities, according to thy most gracious {f.99v} will, for the sake of thy most holy name Jesus, which is blessed for ever. Amen.

## Ambrose, Prayer 2 before Mass *Ambro. Precatio II. ad Missam*

O Lord, if thou hast foreseen that my wickednesses must be avenged with a temporal scourge, with tears I implore thee that, while in anger thou ragest against them, thou wouldest ever in wrath remember mercy. For whom thou lovest thou chastenest, and scourgest every son whom thou receivest. By which words, surely, though the rod of smiting threateneth, yet the fatherly affection of lovingkindness is shewn. Grant, I pray, endurance amid the scourges, that I incur not, as an evil servant, the mark of murmuring, but rather, as a good son, render thanksgivings. Amen.

## Cyprian

O Lord Jesus Christ, who hast made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is,[^9] and who beholdest the depth of the abyss; who didst walk with dry feet upon the waves {f.100r} of the sea, and didst lift up Peter with thy hand when he was sinking, and didst thrice deliver Paul from shipwreck: deliver us from the great billows of this sea, that living we may bless thy name for ever. Vouchsafe also, O Lord, to send thy holy Angel from the heavens, that he may be the guide of our life, as he was of old unto our fathers, when thou broughtest them out of the land of Egypt with great strength and with thy mighty hand. And bring us by a straight way unto the desired place, into thy kingdom, where with the Father and the Holy Ghost thou livest and reignest, God, for ever and ever. Amen.

## The Church

O most merciful Redeemer, who art ever merciful, ever a Saviour, whether thou sendest us sorrowful things or glad: for great is thy mercy, when by outward afflictions, as by bitter medicines, thou healest the inner man, and by temporal troubles preparest us for everlasting joys; {f.100v} forasmuch as thou thyself hast marked out for us with thine own footsteps this way to true blessedness: grant that I may drink this cup, as held out by thee, patiently and obediently. These things indeed are grievous to nature; but thou also hast suffered heavier things for me, and I have deserved things far heavier, who have so often deserved hell. Yet thou knowest the frailty of man’s condition, and therefore, like that merciful Samaritan, thou pourest into our wounds the wine that biteth away our faults, but addest the oil of thy consolation, whereby we may bear the things that to us are unbearable. If it seem good unto thee to add to my pains, add also to the gift of patience, that being preserved I may praise thee, and glorify thy name for ever and ever. Amen.

## A Prayer under the Cross, Gathered out of the Psalms *Precatio sub cruce, ex Psalmis collecta*

Hear me when I call, O God of my righteousness: thou that comfortest me in distress, have mercy upon me, and hear my prayer.

{f.101r} Unto thee will I cry, O Lord my rock; be not silent to me: lest, if thou be silent, I become like them that go down into the pit.

Bow down thine ear to me; make haste to deliver me: be thou my strong rock, and an house of defence to save me.

For thou art my rock and my fortress; and for thy name’s sake thou teachest me and guidest me.

Have mercy upon me, O Lord, for I am in trouble: mine eye is consumed for anger, yea, my soul and my belly.

For my life is spent with grief, and my years with sighing.

Arise, O Lord; save me, O my God. Arise, O Lord God, and let thy hand be lifted up: forget not the poor.

Turn thee unto me, and have mercy upon me; for I am desolate and afflicted.

The troubles of my heart are enlarged: O bring thou me out of my distresses.

O keep my soul, and deliver me: let me not be ashamed; for I have put my trust in thee.

{f.101v} Keep me as the apple of the eye, hide me under the shadow of thy wings.

Hide not thy face from me; put not thy servant away in thine anger.

Be thou my help; leave me not, neither forsake me, O God of my salvation.

Remove thy strokes away from me: I am consumed by the chastening of thine hand.

Hide not thy face from thy servant; for I am in trouble: hear me speedily.

Draw nigh unto my soul, and redeem it. Thou knowest my reproach, and my shame.

O God, be not far from me: O my God, make haste for my help.

Preserve my soul; for I am holy: O thou my God, save thy servant that trusteth in thee.

For mine eyes are unto thee, O Lord: in thee have I trusted; pour not out my soul.

For thy name’s sake, O Lord, thou shalt quicken me: and for thy righteousness’ sake thou shalt bring my soul out of trouble.

Deliver me in thy righteousness, and cause me to escape: {f.102r} be thou my strong rock, whereunto I may continually resort, thou who hast promised to save me.

Unto thee my heart hath said, Seek ye my face: thy face, Lord, will I seek.

How long wilt thou forget me, O Lord? for ever? how long wilt thou hide thy face from me?

How long shall I take counsel in my soul, having sorrow in my heart daily?

Mine eyes have languished by reason of affliction: I have called upon thee, O Lord; all the day I have stretched out my hands unto thee.

Wilt thou shew wonders to the dead? shall the dead arise and praise thee?

Shall any declare thy lovingkindness in the grave? or thy faithfulness in destruction?

Shall thy wonders be known in the dark? and thy righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

Lord, why castest thou off my soul? why hidest thou thy face from me?

{f.102v} In death there is none that remembereth thee: and in the grave who shall give thee thanks?[^10]

Be silent unto God, O my soul; for my expectation is from him. For he is my rock and my saviour, and I am not moved.

In God is my salvation and my glory: the rock of my strength; my hope is in God.

For thy name’s sake, O Lord, thou shalt quicken me: and for thy righteousness’ sake thou shalt bring my soul out of trouble.

O Lord, truly I am thy servant; I am thy servant, and the son of thine handmaid.[^11]

I will offer to thee the sacrifice of thanksgiving; I will pay my vows unto thee in the presence of all the people. Amen, Amen.

## Thanksgiving for Deliverance from the Cross and Affliction *Gratiarum actio pro liberatione ex cruce et afflictione*

I waited patiently for the Lord; and he inclined unto me, and heard my prayers.

{f.103r} And he brought me up out of the pit of misery, and out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings.

I will praise thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will shew forth all thy marvellous works.

I will be glad and rejoice in thee: I will sing praise to thy name, O thou most High.

I will be glad and rejoice in thy mercy: for thou hast considered my trouble; thou hast known my soul in adversity.

In my distress I called upon the Lord, and cried unto my God: he heard my voice out of his holy temple, and my cry came before him, even into his ears.

He sent from above, he took me, he drew me out of many waters.

Who is like unto the Lord our God, who dwelleth on high, and beholdeth the things that are lowly in heaven and in the earth?

Blessed be the Lord daily: he layeth a burden upon us, and yet he is our salvation.

{f.103v} Many are the afflictions of the righteous: and the Lord will deliver them out of them all.

He keepeth all their bones: not one of them shall be broken.

I will bless the Lord at all times: his praise shall continually be in my mouth.

My soul shall make her boast in the Lord: that the afflicted may hear thereof, and be glad.

O magnify the Lord with me, and let us exalt his name together.

I sought the Lord, and he heard me, and delivered me from all my fears.

They that look unto him, and flow together unto him, their faces shall not be ashamed.

The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear him, and shall deliver them.

O taste and see that the Lord is good: blessed is the man that trusteth in him.

Give glory unto God: his excellency is over Israel, and his strength is in the clouds.

{f.104r} God is wonderful in his saints: he is the God of Israel; he shall give strength and power unto his people. Blessed be God.
