- p.252 (img 0310) — "mitte mihi in adiutorium sanctum Angelum" is rendered "send to my help thy holy Angel". The Latin has no "tuum"; strictly "a holy Angel" / "the holy Angel". Left as a defensible supply of the possessive. The same holds for "per crucem et sanguinem" = "by thy cross and blood" in the same prayer.
