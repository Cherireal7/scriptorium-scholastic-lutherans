# Audit log — 03-III-V.md (Sections III, IV, V; printed pp. 36–84, images 0094–0142)

## Changes

- p.37 (img 0095) — Latin: "Spes mea CHRISTE Deus, hominum tu dulcis amator" — was: "My hope, O Christ, my God, thou sweet lover of men" — now: "My hope, O Christ, O God, thou sweet lover of men" — kind: addition
- p.53 (img 0111) — Latin: "equita super verbum veritatis, & afflictionis, propter iusticiam" (Ps. 45) — was: "ride upon the word of truth, and of meekness, for righteousness’ sake" — now: "ride upon the word of truth, and of affliction, for righteousness’ sake" — kind: mistranslation (KJV word kept where the Latin differs)
- p.60 (img 0118), footnote 3 — Latin: "Tu super aquas solutus eas, nomen tuum expandisti Spiritus" ... "Tu qui omnium seculorum sanctos tui nominis docuisti" — was: "and *tui nominis* four lines below" — now: "and *tui nominis* seven verses below (in *Tu qui omnium seculorum*)" — kind: note (the second reading stands seven versicles after the first, not four)

## Doubtful, not changed

- p.44 (img 0102) — heading "Cap. 31.2." is rendered "chapter 31.2". The print does read "31.2."; it may be meant for "31. 32." (chapters 31 and 32). Left as printed.
- p.49 (img 0107) — "sed ob illam ... humanitatem, minor ea" is rendered "thou art less than it". "ea" can be read as ablative of comparison (less than the divine nature) or as "by it" (less by reason of the manhood, that is, less than the Father). Either is possible; left.
- p.49 (img 0107) — "Nonne es caro & frater meus? Vere sic famem habuisti" is rendered "Truly thus thou didst hunger"; it could also be divided "Truly so. Thou didst hunger". Left.
- p.60–61 (img 0118–0119) — *Veni sancte Spiritus*: the print sets each stanza as one run-on paragraph; the English sets three lines to a stanza. Nothing is missing or added; this is layout only, so left.
- p.63 (img 0121) — "Qui te in eiusdem &c." (so the print, for "Qui tecum in unitate eiusdem") is rendered "Who with thee, in the unity of the same, etc." The expansion of the formula is certain; left.
- p.66 (img 0124) — "tremunt super se, & super hominem Deum" is rendered "tremble at one above themselves, even at the man who is GOD". The print's "& super" (the usual text has "super se hominem Deum") is smoothed, the sense is kept; left.
- p.70 (img 0128) — Ps. 72: "Descendit sicut pluvia" is rendered in the present ("He cometh down"), where the KJV has the future; "dabit ei de auro Arabiae" is rendered in the passive with the KJV. Both defensible; left.
- p.67 (img 0125) — the second prayer of Section V is headed "Cap. 15." in the print, as the first is (it is chapter 16 in the usual numbering). The English follows the print; left.
