# Musculus, Precationes — collation of the 1561 and 1573 editions

Date of collation: 2026-11-26.

- **1561** = Frankfurt on the Oder 1561, `sources/musculus-precationes/bsb-1561/` (BSB Asc. 5561 x). Foliated, leaves 1–139. Leaf N recto = image 13 + 2N; leaf N verso = image 14 + 2N.
- **1573** = Leipzig 1573, `sources/musculus-precationes/bsb-1573/` (BSB Asc. 5554 s). Paginated, printed page N = image N + 58.

## Method

1. Line-level comparison of the two OCR texts (long s, u/v, i/j, ae/e normalised; 7-letter shingles), to find which 1573 lines have no counterpart in 1561 and the reverse.
2. A word-level alignment of the whole prayer text of both editions (1561: 33,584 words; 1573: 38,739 words). It returned exactly ten blocks present in 1573 and absent from 1561, and no block of printed text present in 1561 and absent from 1573. A moved piece would have shown as a deletion in one place and an insertion in another; none appeared.
3. Each of the ten blocks was then split into its pieces by heading. Headings and incipits were read on the 1573 page images for images 72–73, 92–93, 121, 122, 138, 140–141, 168–170, 172–173, 175, 177, 179–180, 191, 250, 252, 303, 317–320; the rest of the incipits are from the OCR, checked against the translation's headings (same number of pieces in every stretch).
4. Absence from 1561 was confirmed by a fuzzy search of every incipit through the whole 1561 OCR. Six incipits were found elsewhere in 1561; they are marked "repeat" in part B.
5. Wording was compared on the page images for 18 prayers, at least one from every section but XIII (part D).

**Front and back matter**

- **Title.** 1561 already reads "… nunc recens [re]cognitae et auctae. Per Andream Musculum Doctorem. Francofordiae ad Oderam. 1561" (image 0005). It is itself a revised and enlarged edition.
- **Preliminaries differ completely.** 1561 has a dedication to Duke Johann Albrecht of Mecklenburg (images 0007–0014), dated 3 March 1561, in which Musculus calls this "hac tertia editione" (image 0008). 1573 has a "Praefatio" (images about 0047–0057) dated Frankfurt on the Oder, vigil of Mary Magdalene, 1570. The OCR texts of the two share almost nothing (under 12 % of shingles on every page but one), so they are different texts, not a revision of one another.
- **Calendar.** 1561 has none. 1573 has a calendar with month verses and weather prognostics (images about 0020–0046).
- **Index.** 1561 has no printed index. After leaf 139 the 1561 copy has only manuscript leaves: an owner's handwritten "Regestrum" of hymns and psalms with leaf numbers (image 0292 onward) and unrelated notes. 1573 has the printed "Index precationum omnium" (from image 0321).

## B. Pieces found only in 1573

94 items in ten stretches, one stretch at the end of each of sections I, II, IV, V, VI, VII, X, XI, XV and XVIII (in XI and XV just before the closing thanksgiving). By word count they are 5,169 of 38,739 words, 13.3 % of the 1573 prayer text, about 35 printed pages; measured by the page spans below, about 40 pages of 262.

Six of the 94 repeat text that 1561 has in another place (marked **repeat**); 88 are text 1561 does not have at all.

Sections III, VIII, IX, XII, XIII, XIV, XVI and XVII received no additions.

## C. Found only in 1561, or placed differently

**Nothing printed in the 1561 prayer text is missing from 1573, and no piece stands in a different place.** The word-level alignment found no deleted block and no transposition; every 1561 piece from leaf 1r to leaf 139r has its counterpart in 1573 in the same order.

The seven 1561 images that the earlier automatic comparison matched only partly (0030, 0034, 0110, 0113, 0167, 0222, 0263) hold no extra text:

- 0034 (leaf 10v): seven lines of an owner's handwriting in the lower margin, which the OCR read as text.
- 0030, 0110, 0113, 0167, 0222, 0263: poor OCR at page edges and line ends; the printed text is in 1573 (0263 was read on the image, see D no. 14).

Differences that are not additions:

1. **One attribution dropped.** 1561 leaf 137v (image 0288) heads the second table prayer "CHRYSOSTO. IBIDEM: GRATIARUM actio"; 1573 p. 260 has only "GRATIARUM ACTIO".
2. **Running heads.** In 1561 the thanksgiving psalms after Communion in section IX carry their own running head "GRATIARUM ACTIONES" (leaves 89v–94r, images 0192–0201); it is not a separate section. 1561 has the running head "PRECATIONES PRO / VITA AETERNA" by mistake over the last opening of the evening prayers (leaves 136v–137r, images 0286–0287); 1573 has the same kind of slip ("PRO VITA" on p. 252, image 0310, in section XVII).
3. **Six texts printed once in 1561 are printed twice in 1573** (part B, nos. 2, 19 in part, 30, 32, 53, 65).
4. **Preliminaries, calendar, index:** see part A.
5. **The copy.** In the 1561 copy an owner struck through "Hagios, Hagios, Hagios" in Cyprian's prayer (leaf 95r, image 0203) and wrote "Sancte, sancte, sancte" beside it. The print reads "Hagios" in both editions.

**Result.** The shared prayers are the same text. 1573 is a reprint of the 1561 text with small verbal changes of one or two words: some correct 1561 misprints, some are new misprints, a few are deliberate retouches ("fieret" → "esset", "ac" → "oramus", "quem" → "super quem", "quousque" → "usque quo"). In the 18 prayers checked there are 7 such variants in the opening sentences, so a full collation would probably turn up a variant of this size every one or two pages. None found changes the sense of a prayer.

## E. What could not be determined

1. **Incipits not read on the image.** In part B the incipits of nos. 18, 41–42, 45, 47–48, 50–51, 54–57, 59–70, 73–76, 81–88, 90 and 92 are from the OCR, normalised by me, not read letter by letter on the image. The headings and the count of pieces agree with the translation in every stretch. In no. 46 the print has a damaged letter ("vir.us").
2. **Small variants over the whole book.** Only 18 prayers were compared on the images. The OCR alignment cannot tell a one-letter variant from an OCR error, so the full list of small verbal variants is not established. The alignment did flag about 270 places; nearly all are running heads, catchwords and OCR noise, and the ones that looked real were checked and are listed in D.
3. **One doubtful reading.** 1561 leaf 33r (image 0079), "nihil ā / immundanorum": the line end is damaged in the scan and I could not decide between "iam immundanorum" and a misprint of "iam mundanorum".
4. **The 1573 preliminaries.** The calendar and preface of 1573 were not collated beyond establishing that 1561 has neither; their exact image limits (calendar about 0020–0046, preface about 0047–0057) were taken from the OCR, not checked page by page.
5. **Earlier editions.** 1561 calls itself the third edition and "recens recognitae et auctae". Which of the 1573 additions first appeared between 1561 and 1573 (the 1573 preface is dated 1570), and what 1561 itself added to the editions before it, cannot be told from these two copies.
6. **Completeness of the 1561 copy.** The foliation runs 1–139 without a gap in the images and the text ends with "AMEN" on 139r, so the prayer text is complete. Whether the copy once had further printed leaves after 139 (an index, a colophon) is not visible; the following leaves are manuscript.
7. **Sources of the added pieces.** Not investigated; the note that nos. 37–51 are the "Fifteen Oes" is from the incipits alone.
