## Changes

- August Cisiojanus (img 0032) — Latin: "Pe, Steph, Steph, Prothus, Six" — was: "Peter, Stephen, Stephen, Protus, Sixtus" — now: "Peter, Stephen, Stephen the Protomartyr, Sixtus" — kind: mistranslation (the syllables Pro-thus fall on 4–5 August and belong to Stephen the protomartyr of 3 August; Protus is 11 September)
- 27 August (img 0033) — Latin: "Rufini & Gebhardi" — was: "Rufus and Gebhard" — now: "Rufinus and Gebhard" — kind: misreading
- December closing verse (img 0041) — Latin: "Iam vellit Barbam Niclas" — was: "Now Nicholas plucketh at Barbara," — now: "Now Nicholas plucketh the beard [Barbara]," — kind: mistranslation (barbam = beard, a play on Barbara)
- 20 November (img 0039) — Latin: "Edmun. reg. & mag." — was: "Edmund, King, and *mag.* [?]" — now: "Edmund, King and Martyr" with new footnote 7 stating the print's reading — kind: note ([?] resolved; the reading "mag." is clear, a misprint for "mar.")
- 24 September (img 0035) — Latin: "Ando. Tit. & Fe. mar." — was: "Andochius, Thyrsus [?] and Felix, Martyrs" — now: "[?]" removed — kind: note ([?] resolved; Andochius, Thyrsus and Felix is the feast of 24 September)
- May Cisiojanus (copy2 img 0032) — Latin: "in pede Cris, Can." — was: "Crispulus [?], Cantius [?]" — now: "Crispulus [?], Cantius" — kind: note (second [?] resolved; Can falls on 31 May, the Cantiani)
- Qualities of the four parts (img 0044) — Latin: "calidum Ver sitit humensque" — was: "spring is warm and moist [?]." — now: "[?]" replaced by new footnote 8 giving the print's reading — kind: note ([?] resolved; "sitit" reads clearly, a misprint)
