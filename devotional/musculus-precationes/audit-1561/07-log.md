# Log — 1561 edition, sections XII–XV (file 07-XII-XV.md)

Range: from the Section XII heading on leaf 104r (image 0221) to the end of Section XV on leaf 129v (image 0272, last line "…reddam pro salute mea Domino"). The Section XVI heading stands at the foot of 129v and belongs to the next part. Leaf 104r begins with the last three lines of Section XI, so the marker `{f.104r}` belongs in the Section XI file; the first marker here is `{f.104v}`.

## Left out

1. **Prayer (*Oratio*)**, "O Lord Jesus Christ, I ask for thy Paradise, not for any worth of my merits" (*Domine Iesu Christe, Paradysum tuum postulo*), with its **Verse** "Thou hast loosed my bonds, O Lord" (1573 p. 245).
2. **Another (*Alia*)**, "O Lord Jesus Christ, for that bitterness which thou didst endure for me" (1573 pp. 245–246).

In 1561 the psalm prayer ends on leaf 128v ("…placebo Domino in regione vivorum") and the "Gratiarum actio pro liberatione ex morbo" follows at once on the same page. Nothing else in sections XII–XV is missing from 1561.

### Small capitals

The 1561 print has no small capitals inside the text in this range (it prints plain "Deus", "Domine" where 1573 has DEVS, DOMINE). Under the convention "Deus in small capitals = GOD", every GOD and LORD of the 1573 file is therefore given here as God and Lord (18 places: 11 LORD, 7 GOD).

## Doubtful

- 124r "Taedet animam vitae meae": taken as an omission of "meam" (Job 10:1) and the English left as "My soul"; a strictly literal rendering would be "The soul is weary of my life".
- 121r "clave crucis tuae": taken as a misprint of "clavo" (helm, tiller), which the nautical image requires; if "clave" is meant, the sense is "with the key of thy cross".
- 111r "laudaris", 115v "multa plura", 124r "dilecta mi", 124v "ad in locum": treated as slips of the 1561 compositor, English unchanged.
- Split words at a page turn (114r pre-tiosi, 114v proce-dunt, 116v Quae-so, 127r quoni-am): the marker stands before the English word or clause that renders the split word.
- 125v: the page turns between "fari" and "non possum"; marker set as "when I can speak {f.125v} no more".
- No word on any page of the range was illegible.
