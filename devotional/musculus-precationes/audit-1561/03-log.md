## Doubtful

1. Leaf 26r, heading "AVGVST. SOLLILOQUIO. CA. 31. II.": the last two characters are the same sort as the "1" in "31", so the print can be read "31. 11" or "31. II". 1573 prints "31.2", which is the reading kept in English.
2. Leaf 34v, "in Columbae specie & super sanctos discipulos apparuisti, in linguis igneis descendisti": this may be a transposition by the 1561 printer that 1573 set right; it can be construed, so it was translated as printed.
3. Leaf 34r, "munera docuisti": may be a misprint for *munere*; construable, translated as printed.
4. Small capitals. 1561 sets *Deus* and *Dominus* in ordinary type throughout these sections; the "GOD" and "LORD" of the 1573 English (outside the psalms) reflect 1573's small capitals. They were left as they stand, pending one decision for the whole book.
5. Section V title: 1561 spells "FORMULAE PRAECANDI"; the title line keeps *precandi*, as in the collation table.
6. Leaf 32v, hymn *Quaeramus puro corde*: 1561 reads "Memento quod sumus oves tuae"; the 1573 OCR has "simus" (not checked on the 1573 image; the English is the same either way).
7. Page markers at a word divided across two pages are set next to the English word that renders it (21v, 22r, 23r, 23v, 24v, 25r, 26r, 30r, 32r, 37v, 38v, 39r).
