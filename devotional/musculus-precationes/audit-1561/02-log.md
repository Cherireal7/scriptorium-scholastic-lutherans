## Doubtful

1. 13r *Allice*: the received text of the Soliloquies has *Allide*, as 1573. *Allice* may be a 1561 misprint (c for d) and not a reading. It is a real word and gives good sense, so it is translated as printed ("Allure") with a footnote. If it is judged a misprint, restore "Break my desire in pieces" and reword footnote 5.
2. 3r *eris ante omnia* and 2r *confitentem* are treated as misprints because they give no good sense; a reader who wants the print word for word would have "shalt be before all things" and "confess in thy mercy alone".
3. Page markers where the page break falls inside a word or where English reverses the Latin order are set at the nearest word boundary: 1v and 2v (*tri-num*), 6r (*preca-mur*), 8r (*di-vinitatis*), 11r (*in-terpellans*), 12r (*perdu-cat*), 12v (*Opera manuum tuarum / ne despicias*), 13v (*concupi-scam*), 14r (*a / te recepi*), 15r (*Sanctificator / meus*), 16v (*reg-nat*), 18v (*timenti-bus*), 19r (*Domi-nus*), 19v (*decem / millia*), 20v (*ope-ra tua*).
4. 9r Te Deum: both editions print *quos precioso sanguine redemisti* without *tuo*; the English "with thy precious blood" is left.
5. Handwriting ignored: the owner's lines at the foot of 10v (img 0034) and in its margin; a word overwritten in ink at 8r (*resurgere habent*).
