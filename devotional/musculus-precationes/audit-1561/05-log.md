# Log — sections VII and VIII, strict 1561 edition

File: `translation-1561/05-VII-VIII.md`. Source: 1561 leaves 61v–83r (images 0136–0179). Section IX begins at the top of leaf 83v (image 0180).

Every 1561 page image of the range was read against the 1573 English. The 1573 Latin of all the prose prayers (1573 images 0180–0182, 0195–0216) was also read beside the 1561 Latin.

## Left out

Section VII in 1561 ends with Psalm 110 on leaf 66r; the heading of section VIII follows on the same page. The ten pieces that 1573 adds after Psalm 110 (pp. 129–137) are not in 1561:

1. Hymn, *Ad coenam agni providi*
2. Hymn, *Vita sanctorum, decus angelorum*
3. Hymn, *Festum nunc celebre*
4. Sequence on Easter Day, *Victimae paschali laudes*
5. Sequence on the Ascension of the Lord, *Rex omnipotens die hodierna*
6. Benedicamus, *Quando Christus ascenderat*
7. Collect, “O God, who by the Paschal solemnity” (*Deus qui solennitate paschali*), with the verse “This is the day which the Lord hath made”
8. Another, “Pour into us, O Lord, the Spirit of thy charity” (*Spiritum nobis Domine tuae charitatis infunde*)
9. Another, “Grant, we beseech thee, almighty God, that we who have kept the Paschal feast” (*Praesta quaesumus … festa paschalia*)
10. Gregory, “O Lord Jesus Christ, I adore thee hanging on the cross” (six short prayers)

Old footnotes 1 and 2 (on *a nobis* and *exaltemus*) fell away with these pieces. Section VIII has no piece left out.

The three places named by the second reader of the 1573 text:

- *desperando respirem* (leaf 69v): 1561 prints the same. English kept; footnote says both prints have it and that it is probably a misprint for *sperando respirem*.
- *confidendo totiens* (leaf 74r): 1561 prints *confitendo*. English changed (table above).
- *consignato corpore claritatis tuae* (leaf 77r): 1561 prints *corpori*. English changed (table above).

Misprints of 1561 where the right reading is translated and the printed one is given in a footnote:

- 61v *qui mori dignatus est* for *es* (1573 has *es*).
- 71v *imungitis* for *inungitis*.
- 73r heading *de interiori clamo* for *domo* (1573 has the same misprint; old footnote kept).
- 74v *via mea* for *vita mea* (1573 has *vita*).
- 77r *tenete … inevitabilis iustitia* for *terrente … inevitabili iustitia*; *contemptor* for *contemplor* (1573 has the right forms).
- 81v *oculis circuit* for *osculis* (1573 has the same; old footnote kept).

Small misprints of 1561 that do not touch the English, not footnoted: 63r *declaristi*; 67r *Quando namque* (for *Quanto*), *redimerit*; 68r *filii Dei vivi* (for *fili*); 70r catchword *Magnus* (for *Manus*); 72v *irretiam*; 76v *cupiditati foedavit*; 78v *clemenaia*; 81v *vuguendo*; 82r *pro censecuta*. Leaf 75v *pro me sudisti sanguinem* stands for *sudasti* (long s, checked enlarged), so “didst sweat” is kept.

Other changes:

- Small capitals. 1573 sets *DOMINVS*, *DEVS* and the like in small capitals in many places; 1561 prints them in ordinary type throughout this range. Every “LORD” and “GOD” of the 1573 English is therefore “Lord” and “God” here (Psalms 68, 110 and 118; the prayers on leaves 69v, 70v, 71r, 72v, 74v, 75r; the two psalm centos). No footnotes.
- Section headings as 1561 prints them: *… Resurrectionis et ascensionis in Coelum …*; *Formulae precandi, poenitentiam agentium* (with the comma). Hymn incipit *Christe qui es creator et redemptor* (1561 spelling).
- Page markers `{f.62r}` to `{f.83r}`, 43 markers. Leaf 61v begins in section VI (end of the hymn *Rex Christe factor omnium*), so `{f.61v}` belongs in the file for section VI and is not in this file.

## Doubtful

- Leaf 74v, *Infirmata est in paupertate via mea*. Taken as a misprint for *vita mea* (so 1573; “my life” kept, with a footnote). It could be translated as printed, “my way”.
- Leaf 74v, *et hoc nunc et imperpetuum*. Translated as printed (“both now and for evermore”). It may be only a misprint for *ex hoc nunc*, which 1573 has.
- Leaf 74v, *Ego per unigeniti …* and *crucifixit ad mortem*. Both translated as printed; either could be a slip that 1573 corrected (*Ergo*, *et mortem*).
- Leaf 67r, *seu*. The 1561 reading gives the sense “the east is not so far from the west, nor hell from heaven, as these two are apart”. The new English follows that; the sentence is hard in both prints.
- Leaf 69v, *desperando respirem*: the suggested right reading *sperando respirem* was not checked against an edition of the source.
- Small capitals: whether the other parts of the 1561 edition also drop “LORD” and “GOD” should be made to agree across the book.
- Not changed, though a little free against both prints: 67r “the righteousness of my Redeemer” (*redemptoris iustitia*, no *mei*); 77r “according to thy promises, which thou must needs fulfil” (*secundum promissa tua, necesse est ut impleas*, no relative).
