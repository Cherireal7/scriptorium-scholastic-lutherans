## Doubtful

1. Leaf 97r, *Vincis cor meum, ut timeat nomen tuum* (Ps 86:11). The word is clear on the image. It can be read as it stands (*vincis* from *vincire*, "thou bindest"), and I translated it so, with the 1573 reading *Unias* in footnote 7. It may equally be a compositor's misreading of *Unias* (or of *Vinci*, "bind"). If the editor prefers to treat it as a misprint, restore "Unite my heart to fear thy name." and reword footnote 7 as a misprint note.
2. Leaf 98v, *non temerariis moribus agitur*. Treated as a misprint for *motibus* (the reading of 1573 and of the source text, Augustine's Meditations), so "chance motions" stands, with footnote 8. *moribus* gives a possible but weaker sense ("is not led by reckless ways"). The collation (part D, no. 11) lists it as a variant, not as a misprint.
3. Page marker `{f.84v}`: the Latin page turns between *voluptatis* and *tuae inebria illud*; the English word order is reversed ("make it drunk with the plenty of thy pleasure"), so the marker stands before "thy pleasure", one or two words off.
4. Not touched, because they are the same in both editions and not errors of 1573 against 1561: "this mystery" for *posuisti mysterium* (86r, no *istud* in either print); "that with a sound palate I may be able to eat thee" for *ut sano palato possis manducari a me* (84v).
