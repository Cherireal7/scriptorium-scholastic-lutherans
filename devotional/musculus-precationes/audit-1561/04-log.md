## Doubtful

- 50r *clarissimus natus*: the usual text of the Meditations has *carissimus*, so the 1561 reading may itself be a misprint that 1573 corrected. It makes sense as it stands, so it is translated as printed ("most glorious") with a footnote.
- 48v *eculeum* / 1573 *aculeum*: *eculeum* ("rack") is the usual text; treated as a true variant, not as a 1573 misprint, since *aculeum* also gives sense and the 1573 English translated it.
- 58r *innocens* / *innocuus*: nearly the same in sense; rendered "innocent" against "guiltless" only so that the two editions differ in English as in Latin.
- 53v marker: the page turns between *Deus* and *meus*; the marker stands just after "the Lord my God is;".
- Footnote 8 records a difference of layout, not of words; remove it if layout differences are not to be noted.
