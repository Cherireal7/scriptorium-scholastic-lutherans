# Modern Critical Editions Registry

*Reference list of standard modern editions used across the Scholastic Lutherans corpus for endnote-citation purposes. Grow as needed.*

## Patristics — Greek

- **PG** — Migne, *Patrologia Graeca* (161 vols, 1857–66). Cite vol:col.
- **GCS** — *Die griechischen christlichen Schriftsteller* (Berlin/Leipzig, 1897–). Newer critical.
- **SC** — *Sources chrétiennes* (Paris: Cerf, 1942–). Critical + French translation.
- **CCSG** — *Corpus Christianorum, Series Graeca* (Turnhout: Brepols).
- **NPNF** — *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers* (Schaff, ed., 28 vols, 1886–1900). English.

## Patristics — Latin

- **PL** — Migne, *Patrologia Latina* (221 vols, 1844–55). Cite vol:col.
- **CCSL** — *Corpus Christianorum, Series Latina* (Turnhout: Brepols). Newer critical.
- **CSEL** — *Corpus Scriptorum Ecclesiasticorum Latinorum* (Vienna, 1866–). Critical.
- **SC** — as above (also carries Latin fathers).
- **ANF** — *Ante-Nicene Fathers* (Roberts/Donaldson, 10 vols). English.

## Luther

- **WA** — *Weimarer Ausgabe* / *D. Martin Luthers Werke* (Weimar: Böhlau, 1883–2009). Cite vol:pg (or WA-Br for correspondence, WA-Tr for Table Talk, WA-DB for German Bible).
- **LW** — *Luther's Works*, American Edition (Pelikan/Lehmann/Wengert, 82 vols, St. Louis: Concordia, 1955–). Cite vol:pg.

## Melanchthon

- **CR** — *Corpus Reformatorum* (Bindseil/Bretschneider, 28 vols, 1834–60) — vols 1–28 are Melanchthon. Cite vol:col.
- **MSA** — *Melanchthons Werke in Auswahl*, ed. Stupperich (7 vols, Gütersloh, 1951–75). Cite vol:pg.

## Confessional Documents (Lutheran)

- **BSLK** — *Die Bekenntnisschriften der evangelisch-lutherischen Kirche* (Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1930). Standard 20th-c. edition.
- **BSELK** — *Die Bekenntnisschriften der Evangelisch-Lutherischen Kirche* (Göttingen: V&R, 2014). Newer critical.
- **Kolb-Wengert** — *The Book of Concord*, ed. Kolb & Wengert (Minneapolis: Fortress, 2000). Standard modern English.
- **Tappert** — *The Book of Concord*, ed. Tappert (Fortress, 1959). Older English; still cited.
- **Triglot** — *Concordia Triglotta* (St. Louis: Concordia, 1921). Latin/German/English parallel.

## Reformed comparanda

- **Calvin, *Institutes*** — Beveridge or Battles translation. Cite Bk:Ch:§.
- **CR (Reformed)** — vols 29–101 of *Corpus Reformatorum* = Calvin's Works.
- **Heppe** — *Reformed Dogmatics*, ed. Heppe/Bizer, tr. Thomson (Grand Rapids: Baker, 1950). Reformed sources in English translation.

## Roman Catholic comparanda

- **Denzinger** — *Enchiridion Symbolorum* (43rd ed., Freiburg: Herder, 2010). Cite DH-number.
- **Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae*** — Leonine ed. Cite Pt-Q-Art.
- **Suarez** — Vives ed. (28 vols, Paris, 1856–78).
- **Bellarmine** — *Opera omnia* (Naples 1856–62 or Paris 1870–74).

## Classical

- **LCL** — *Loeb Classical Library* (Harvard). Default for classical Greek/Latin.
- **OCT** — *Oxford Classical Texts*.
- **TLL** — *Thesaurus Linguae Latinae* (for lexicographical questions).
- **LSJ** — Liddell-Scott-Jones (for Greek lexicography).
- **PMG** — *Poetae Melici Graeci*, ed. Page (Oxford, 1962).

## Bible

- **BHS / BHQ** — *Biblia Hebraica Stuttgartensia / Quinta*. Hebrew OT.
- **LXX** — Rahlfs/Hanhart Göttingen critical.
- **NA28** — Nestle-Aland *Novum Testamentum Graece*, 28th ed.
- **Vulgate** — Weber-Gryson *Biblia Sacra iuxta Vulgatam Versionem* (Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft).
- **Luther Bible** — WA-DB or the 1912/1984/2017 editions as needed.
- **ESV** — English Standard Version (default English body text).
- **BDB** — Brown-Driver-Briggs, Hebrew lexicon.
- **HALOT** — Koehler-Baumgartner, Hebrew and Aramaic Lexicon.

## Lutheran Orthodoxy — modern reprints and studies

- **Preus** — *The Theology of Post-Reformation Lutheranism*, ed. Preus (2 vols, St. Louis: Concordia, 1970–72). Study, not re-edition; but comprehensive.
- **Fischer** — 19th-c. reprints of Gerhard's *Loci* and Hollaz.
- **Modern facsimile PDFs on PRDL** — often the practical working text; cite the facsimile edition (place, printer, year) parenthetically.

## Bibliographic databases

- **PRDL** — Post-Reformation Digital Library (`prdl.org`). First stop for Reformation-orthodoxy Latin.
- **VD16 / VD17 / VD18** — *Verzeichnis der im deutschen Sprachbereich erschienenen Drucke des 16./17./18. Jahrhunderts*. German-language.
- **RISM** — *Répertoire International des Sources Musicales* (for musical items).
- **Vogel/Einstein/Lesure/Sartori** — *Bibliografia della musica italiana vocale profana* (for Italian vocal music).
